    📰 News & Prophecy¶

    "Prophecies serve as warnings for effects proceeding out of certain bad, evil causes set by you human beings of Earth… if you change the cause toward the good or positive, then the effect also changes. Every thing that exists becomes and passes — change is the only constant, and it begins with the causes you choose."

    — Goblet of the Truth, §110–111, §240

    The law of cause and effect is the most precise and impartial of all creational laws. It does not punish or reward — it reflects. Every thought, feeling, word, and deed generates a specific vibrational cause. That cause accumulates energy, compresses into lived experience, and returns — amplified — as your destiny.

    On this page, we track current world events as observed causes and project their necessary effects if those causes persist unchanged. This is not fortune-telling. It is causal inference — the same principle that lets a physicist predict a trajectory from position and velocity. See the cause, know the effect. Change the cause, and the effect dissolves.

    Updated 2026-07-25

    🔥 France & Spain Wildfires — 250,000 Flee as Climate Reality Burns Through Europe¶

    Observed Cause

    On July 25, 2026, more than 250,000 people have been forced to flee their homes as wildfires rage across southwestern France and northern Spain — a dramatic escalation from the 12,000 evacuated just one week prior. The AP and BBC report that Spanish Prime Minister has warned of "complex hours" ahead, while French authorities say they are "far from bringing the blazes burning near Bordeaux under control." The fires, driven by extreme drought, record temperatures, and high winds, have overwhelmed firefighting resources across both countries. The Guardian reports tourists fleeing "a wall of flames" as entire holiday regions are evacuated. The deeper cause is not the weather — it is the cumulative emission of greenhouse gases over two centuries of industrial civilisation, compounded by inadequate investment in fire prevention, land management, and climate adaptation infrastructure. France — a nuclear-powered, comparatively low-emission economy — is burning anyway because the atmosphere does not respect national carbon accounting. Every nation that has treated climate change as a future problem rather than a present emergency is now experiencing the compaction of that delay.

    Projected Effect (if cause persists)
    • Mass displacement becomes routine — when 250,000 people flee in a single weekend, the effect is a normalisation of climate-driven displacement; Europe will face not only heatwaves but an annual season of evacuation that strains emergency services, housing, and mental health infrastructure
    • Insurance systems crack — when entire regions become uninsurable due to wildfire risk, the effect is a cascading financial crisis where homeowners cannot sell, banks cannot lend, and governments become the insurers of last resort for millions of properties
    • Food production threatened — the Bordeaux region is one of the world's most famous wine-producing areas; the effect of repeated extreme fire seasons is the permanent loss of agricultural zones that have sustained local economies for centuries
    • Political blame games intensify — when a crisis this large hits wealthy nations simultaneously (France, Spain, Canada, Oregon, Scotland), the effect is not automatic cooperation but political finger-pointing; governments will blame each other, blame previous administrations, blame insufficient EU funding — and the underlying cause continues to compound
    • Health crisis from smoke — Calgary's air quality rated among the worst on earth as Canadian wildfire smoke drifts south; the effect is a continent-wide respiratory health emergency that does not respect borders, with emergency room visits for asthma and cardiac events spiking hundreds of miles from the nearest flame

    "Through the accumulation of the energy and power which leads back to the thought-feeling-based cause, an ever greater compaction forms which the creator must experience." — §118

    The hinge: The cause is not a single decision but the accumulated weight of millions of decisions — to burn fossil fuels, to underinvest in adaptation, to prioritise short-term economic growth over long-term survival. The hinge is whether these fires — the largest evacuation in European history — serve as the necessary shock that finally breaks the cycle of inadequate response. It is whether France and Spain invest not only in firefighting capacity but in the systemic changes that reduce fire risk: reforestation with fire-resistant species, building codes that mandate firebreaks, water management systems that function under drought, and — above all — emissions reductions that address the root cause. The hinge is whether a wealthy nation like France, suddenly helpless before a force it cannot control, finally grasps that adaptation without mitigation is a losing strategy. Change the cause — decarbonise the economy, redesign the landscape for fire resilience, treat climate adaptation as national security — and the effect changes from catastrophic loss to managed transition. The fires are not fate; they are the consequence of choices that can still be unmade.

    🏳️‍🌈 Berlin Pride Attack — Van Drives Into Crowd, One Killed¶

    Observed Cause

    On July 25, 2026, a van drove into a crowd at Berlin's Christopher Street Day Pride parade, killing at least one person and injuring several others. The AP reports that police launched an intensive manhunt for the driver, and the parade was immediately called off. The incident occurred in Berlin's city centre, striking participants in what was meant to be a celebration of LGBTQ+ identity and visibility. The specific motive of the attacker is not yet confirmed as of this writing — police have not ruled out terrorism — but the pattern is tragically familiar: a vehicle used as a weapon against a vulnerable community gathered in public. The broader cause is the rising tide of anti-LGBTQ+ rhetoric and violence across Europe and North America, where political movements in multiple countries have made the rights and visibility of queer people a central cultural battleground. When political leaders, media figures, and online influencers dehumanise a group — portraying them as threats to children, to tradition, to national identity — they create a climate in which the most disturbed members of society feel authorised to act.

    Projected Effect (if cause persists)
    • Pride events face heightened security and self-censorship — the effect of attacks like this is not deterrence but adaptation; future Pride events will require vehicle barriers, armed police presence, and intrusive security checks, fundamentally altering the open, celebratory character of these gatherings
    • Political polarisation deepens — attacks on LGBTQ+ events are exploited by both sides: anti-LGBTQ+ movements use them to argue that Pride events are "provocative" targets, while activists use them to argue that hate speech creates hate crimes; the effect is a hardening of positions rather than a search for common ground
    • Copycat risk — vehicle attacks are among the easiest to replicate; the effect of a high-profile successful attack is an elevated risk of similar incidents at other public events, as the method is proven and widely reported
    • Berlin's identity as open city challenged — Berlin has long prided itself on being one of Europe's most tolerant and queer-friendly cities; the effect of an attack on its most visible LGBTQ+ event is a blow to that identity, with potential knock-on effects on tourism, international events, and the city's self-conception
    • Surveillance state expansion — every mass-casualty event is followed by demands for more surveillance, more police powers, more data collection; the effect is a ratchet toward securitisation that erodes the civil liberties of the very communities being attacked

    "If, however, you change the cause toward the good or positive, then the effect also changes." — §110

    The hinge: The observed cause operates at two levels: the individual act of violence and the climate of dehumanisation that precedes it. The hinge at the individual level is policing and prevention — whether authorities detect and disrupt the attacker before they act. But the deeper hinge is whether societies choose to reverse the dehumanisation of LGBTQ+ people in public discourse. When political leaders treat queer people as a threat to be contained, they are, however indirectly, feeding the cause that produces attacks like this. When they treat them as full members of the human community, the cause loses its energy. Change the cause — condemn not just the attack but the rhetoric that precedes it, invest in community safety without militarising public space, affirm that Pride is not provocation but expression — and the effect changes from a cycle of violence and fear to a society that protects all its members.

    🇺🇦 Russia Strikes Drone Exhibition Near Kyiv — 10 Killed in Broad Daylight¶

    Observed Cause

    On July 24, 2026, Russia launched a ballistic missile strike on a drone exhibition near Kyiv, killing at least ten people. The BBC reports that the event was attended by "prominent members of the Ukrainian defence industry" and was hit in the middle of the day — a deliberate strike on a civilian-adjacent gathering of military-technical professionals. The attack comes as Zelensky faces a political crisis over his removal of Defence Minister Mykhailo Fedorov, with protests continuing and commentators suggesting the president has been politically damaged. The broader cause is Russia's strategic decision to escalate strikes on Ukraine's defence industrial base, targeting the very events, people, and facilities that are building the weapons Ukraine uses to defend itself. The strike on the exhibition — a gathering explicitly connected to drone warfare — signals that Russia is prioritising the destruction of Ukraine's domestic weapons production capacity, recognising that Western aid cannot sustain Ukraine indefinitely if its own industrial base is destroyed.

    Projected Effect (if cause persists)
    • Ukraine's defence production disrupted — the targeted killing of defence industry personnel at a public event sends a chilling signal; the effect is a slowdown in Ukraine's domestic drone and weapons production as experts avoid public gatherings, travel less, and operate under greater security constraints
    • Zelensky's political position weakens — the combination of the defence minister's removal and continued Russian strikes creates a perception of strategic disarray; the effect is growing pressure on Zelensky from both domestic critics who demand a harder line and international partners who question the stability of his government
    • Russian targeting doctrine expands — hitting a drone expo in the middle of the day broadens the definition of legitimate military targets; the effect is that more civilian-adjacent spaces — conferences, exhibitions, supply chain facilities — become battlegrounds, blurring the line between combatant and non-combatant
    • Western aid debate intensifies — as Ukraine sustains losses and political turbulence, the effect is renewed debate in Western capitals about whether aid is achieving its objectives; proponents point to Ukraine's need for air defence against precisely this kind of strike; sceptics question whether the aid is reaching the front lines effectively
    • Drone warfare evolution accelerates — the attack on a drone exhibition is a grim irony; the effect is that both sides double down on drone development, recognising that the side with the most advanced unmanned systems will have the advantage in a war that has become a testing ground for autonomous and remotely piloted weapons

    "Cause and effect always belong together, they are one in themselves alone and are inseparable." — §192

    The hinge: The cause is Russia's decision to escalate its targeting of Ukraine's defence industrial ecosystem, and Ukraine's dependence on a war economy that concentrates expertise in vulnerable, targetable locations. The hinge is whether Ukraine can disperse its defence production — moving key personnel, facilities, and exhibitions to harder, more secure locations — or whether the concentration of talent at events like this makes them predictable targets. It is whether Western allies respond with the air defence systems that could protect Ukrainian cities and events from ballistic missile strikes. It is whether Russia's leadership recognises that destroying Ukraine's defence industry does not end the war — it only ensures that when peace comes, Ukraine will be less capable of defending itself against future aggression. The hinge is whether the cycle of strike and retaliation continues to escalate, or whether there exists a diplomatic off-ramp that both sides can choose. Change the cause — protect Ukraine's production capacity, pursue a ceasefire, prioritise defence of civilian life over military advantage — and the effect changes from a grinding war of attrition toward a negotiated resolution.

    💲 Trump Tariffs Escalate — New Levies Take Effect as Trade War Engulfs Allies and Adversaries Alike¶

    Observed Cause

    On July 25, 2026, new Trump tariffs took effect as the administration escalated its trade war on multiple fronts simultaneously. The AP reports that small businesses have filed lawsuits against the new sweeping tariffs, which the Wall Street Journal describes as affecting a broad range of imports. The Hill reports that the tariffs "come back to haunt GOP as elections near," with Republican candidates facing voter anger over rising prices. Most notably, Trump has threatened new tariffs on Canada over wildfire smoke that has drifted across the border — a threat so unprecedented that it signals a radical expansion of tariff policy into environmental grievance. The CBC reports: "Trump vows tariffs to come for Canada over wildfire smoke that has drifted to U.S." The Globe and Mail adds that Trump claimed "the original Gordie Howe bridge deal is dead," contradicting Prime Minister Carney. Meanwhile, Trump also vowed to investigate the EU over fines on US tech companies, expanding the trade war to Europe. The underlying cause is the Trump administration's theory that tariffs are a universal tool — a weapon that can be used to force compliance on trade, migration, environmental policy, and virtually any bilateral grievance. The effect is a global trade system fragmenting into spheres of punitive tariffs, retaliatory measures, and legal chaos.

    Projected Effect (if cause persists)
    • Consumer prices surge — the AP reports "grocery prices biggest jump in 50 years" as tariffs ripple through supply chains; the effect is a cost-of-living crisis that hits low-income households hardest, eroding the purchasing power of American families regardless of political affiliation
    • GOP electoral damage — the Hill reports that tariffs are "haunting" Republicans as midterm elections approach; the effect is a political backlash that could shift control of Congress, as voters punish the party associated with higher prices and economic uncertainty
    • US-Canada relationship at historic low — threatening tariffs over wildfire smoke — an act of nature — signals that no basis for bilateral cooperation is secure; the effect is a permanent Canadian economic reorientation away from the US, accelerated by the conviction that Washington is no longer a reliable partner
    • Global supply chain reconfiguration — businesses cannot plan under a tariff regime that expands based on weather patterns; the effect is an acceleration of nearshoring and diversification away from US-dependent supply chains, as companies seek predictability even at higher cost
    • WTO and international trade architecture weakened — when the world's largest economy treats tariffs as discretionary political tools rather than rule-based instruments, the effect is the collapse of the post-war trade order; smaller economies see no reason to abide by rules that the US does not follow, and the system fragments into bilateral power negotiations

    "Your self-created destiny is always and down to the finest and smallest nuance fair and corresponds to the effects of the creational laws." — §119

    The hinge: The cause is a presidential choice — the belief that tariffs are an effective and expandable tool of statecraft. The hinge is whether US voters communicate through the ballot box that economic stability matters more than trade-war brinkmanship. It is whether Republican members of Congress — facing electoral consequences — apply meaningful checks on tariff authority. It is whether the business community — which has thus far largely accommodated Trump's trade policies — begins to actively oppose them as the costs become unsustainable. It is whether the threatened tariff on Canadian wildfire smoke is so absurd that it forces a reckoning with the limits of tariff policy — or whether the absurd is simply normalised. The Gordie Howe Bridge stands open; the question is whether it carries trade or stands as a monument to a partnership sacrificed to a political theory that has not delivered the results it promised. Change the cause — restore rule-based trade, negotiate rather than threaten, recognise allies as partners rather than adversaries — and the effect changes from economic fragmentation toward stability.

    🇮🇳 India's 'Cockroach' Protests Succeed — Education Minister Resigns, Movement Ends¶

    Observed Cause

    On July 25, 2026, India's Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan resigned, and the Cockroach Janata Party (CJP) protests — the largest student-led movement in years — were called off. The BBC and AP report that the protests, which began over exam paper leaks and swelled into a generational challenge to Prime Minister Narendra Modi's government, achieved their primary objective. The AP describes the movement as "the most visible expression of public anger against PM Narendra Modi's government in recent years." The CJP — whose cockroach symbol represented resilience against efforts to crush it — used memes, social media, and decentralised digital organising to circumvent Modi's tight control over traditional media. Activist Sonam Wangchuk, who had been on a 26-day hunger strike, ended his fast. The resignation of the education minister is a genuine victory for the movement — a rare instance of a sustained protest campaign in Modi's India forcing a change at the ministerial level. The deeper cause remains the same as last week: a generation of young Indians, educated and connected, who feel their future is being sacrificed to nationalist ideology — but the cause has now produced an observable effect.

    Projected Effect (if cause persists)
    • Movement demonstrates that protest works — the resignation is a powerful signal that sustained, digitally-organised protest can achieve concrete political outcomes in Modi's India; the effect is the empowerment of other movements — farmers, workers, regional groups — who now have a template for effective resistance
    • Modi's invincibility myth is cracked — the government's decision to accept the resignation rather than weather the protests is an admission of vulnerability; the effect is a slow erosion of the perception that Modi is politically untouchable, which has been a cornerstone of his authority
    • Education reform now expected — the resignation removes the figurehead but not the underlying problems of exam paper leaks, politicised curricula, and underfunded public education; the effect is that the movement's energy must now shift from protest to policy advocacy, or risk dissipating
    • Digital organising faces new restrictions — the government will have learned from the CJP's use of social media and memes; the effect is likely increased surveillance, algorithmic manipulation, and crackdowns on the digital tools the movement used, even as the movement itself claims victory
    • Generational shift in Indian politics gains momentum — the CJP was Gen Z-led and explicitly rejected traditional party structures; the effect is a demonstration that young Indians can organise outside the political establishment and win — a precedent that could reshape Indian politics in the coming election cycles

    "The destiny which you determine yourselves through your thoughts and feelings, through your activity and your actions and deeds is based on consequences." — §105

    The hinge: The cause changed — and so did the effect. Last week, this page projected that the protests would either force reform or face a crackdown. The education minister's resignation is proof that the hinge works: the protesters' sustained, disciplined, digitally-organised campaign created a cause that the government could not ignore, and the government chose to concede rather than crush. The hinge now is what comes next. The movement has won its stated demand — but the deeper cause — a generation's alienation from an education system that serves nationalist ideology rather than human potential — remains unaddressed. The hinge is whether the CJP transitions from protest to politics — building durable institutions that can advocate for educational reform, hold the new minister accountable, and translate viral energy into sustained policy change. It is whether the government treats the resignation as a genuine reset or a tactical retreat, appointing a reformer or another loyalist. The cockroach survived. The question now is whether it thrives — or merely survives another day. Change the cause — invest in genuine education reform, listen to the young, treat protest as dialogue — and the effect is not just a resigned minister but a transformed system.
