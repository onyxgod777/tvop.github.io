## Independent Measurements — Harry Lear

Harry Lear's channel documents **physical tape measurements** of a 1000&nbsp;mm (one metre) diameter circle: a
CNC-routed disc in European birch, foam-board circles cut with a rotary cutter, and a hardwood circle. He works
with a 4&nbsp;metre Pi Tape Corporation engineering tape, NIST-traceable, graduated in millimetres, with a vernier
attachment read to 0.1&nbsp;mm.

His reported circumference for the one-metre disc is **3144.6&nbsp;mm** — against 3141.59&nbsp;mm for the conventional
constant, a difference of about **3&nbsp;mm**. Sixteen videos, from the introduction through the tape-measurement series
to the geometry and math proofs. Thumbnails are served from this site; nothing contacts YouTube until you press play.
