# The FIGU Contact Reports: Where the Value 4/√φ Actually Comes From

The golden-π movement often cites the FIGU / Contactee contact reports as the origin of the correction of π. It is worth being precise about what those reports actually contain, because the challenge and the specific number are two different things — and only one of them is in the contact reports.

## The challenge is in the reports

The FIGU corpus consistently asserts that Earth's π is wrong. Read against the actual transcripts:

- **Contact Report 251 (3 Feb 1995)** — a single line, embedded in a prophecy about the scientific breakthroughs of "1995 and the following years," states that *"the erroneousness in the calculation of the Pi number"* will be recognized and corrected. It gives **no value** and makes **no technology claim**.
- **Contact Report 260 (3 Feb 1998)** — a member ("Guido") presents a handwritten π calculation, photographed in the report, showing the value π ≈ 3.1446… (about a dozen decimal places) — i.e. the golden value 4/√φ. Ptaah nonetheless declines to confirm the calculation, citing directives that the time is "much too early."
- **Contact Report 712 (28 Nov 2018)** — the strongest statement: π is *"still not exactly and precisely calculated on Earth"* and remains *"erroneous"*; the error is *"minimal-minor"*; and computing the exact value would require an **instrumentarium** that does not yet exist in the earthly-mathematical and instrumental sphere. No value is given.

So the **challenge** — that π is wrong and exactness is beyond current Earth mathematics — is genuinely in the reports.

## The number 4/√φ never appears as a Plejaren declaration

The value **π̂ = 4/√φ = 3.1446055…** never comes from a *direct Plejaren pronouncement* — Ptaah and Quetzal never numerically endorsed any value. In the FIGU record the number appears twice, in each case from a human researcher rather than as a Plejaren statement:

- **Guido's calculation (CR 260, 1998):** the photographed handwritten calculation (German cursive) shows π ≈ 3.1446… (about a dozen decimal places); Ptaah declines to confirm it, citing directives that the time is "much too early."
- **Oleg Kinash's article *"Cosmic Music"* (CR 856, 2023; also Sign of the Times 75):** derives **4 / √1.61803 = 4 / 1.2720… = 3.14460…** from the circle of fifths and Asket's "3, 7, 12"; asserts that *"the first five digits 3.1446 are correct,"* citing **Harry Lear's 2019** measurements; and cautions that even those are *"not sufficiently precise to be considered scientific fact."*

Ptaah's verdict on the Kinash article was a warm but general review — *"very interesting and well written… worth reading and thinking about."* He did not numerically endorse 4/√φ as the exact value.

Independent of the FIGU record, **Harry Lear** arrived at π = 4/√φ through geometric proofs and CNC physical measurement, and **Christian Frehner's** Bulletin 123 (2019) reports physical measurements in the 3.144 range (a 1 m disc coming out ~3 mm over 3.141…, and an acquaintance measuring 3.1441).
