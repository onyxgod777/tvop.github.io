# The FIGU Contact Reports: Where the Value 4/√φ Actually Comes From

The golden-π movement often cites the Billy Meier / FIGU contact reports as the origin of the correction of π. It is worth being precise about what those reports actually contain, because the challenge and the specific number are two different things — and only one of them is in the contact reports.

## The challenge is in the reports

The FIGU corpus consistently asserts that Earth's π is wrong. Read against the actual transcripts:

- **Contact Report 251 (3 Feb 1995)** — a single line, embedded in a prophecy about the scientific breakthroughs of "1995 and the following years," states that *"the erroneousness in the calculation of the Pi number"* will be recognized and corrected. It gives **no value** and makes **no technology claim**.
- **Contact Report 260 (3 Feb 1998)** — a member ("Guido") presents a handwritten π calculation. Ptaah calls it "very amazing" but declines to comment, citing directives that the time is "much too early." The report does not state the value Guido calculated.
- **Contact Report 712 (28 Nov 2018)** — the strongest statement: π is *"still not exactly and precisely calculated on Earth"* and remains *"erroneous"*; the error is *"minimal-minor"*; and computing the exact value would require an **instrumentarium** that does not yet exist in the earthly-mathematical and instrumental sphere. No value is given.

So the **challenge** — that π is wrong and exactness is beyond current Earth mathematics — is genuinely in the reports.

## The number 4/√φ is not a Plejaren declaration

The value **π̂ = 4/√φ = 3.1446055…** does **not** appear in Contact Reports 251, 260, or 712. In the FIGU record it appears in one place: the article *"Cosmic Music"* by **Oleg Kinash (Ukraine)**, which Billy had Ptaah read in **Contact Report 856 (2023)** and which was also published in **FIGU Special Edition Sign of the Times 75**. That article:

- derives **4 / √1.61803 = 4 / 1.2720… = 3.14460…** from the circle of fifths and Asket's "3, 7, 12";
- asserts that *"the first five digits 3.1446 are correct,"* citing **Harry Lear's 2019** circumference measurements;
- and explicitly cautions that even those measurements are *"not sufficiently precise to be considered scientific fact."*

Ptaah's verdict on the article was a warm but general review — *"very interesting and well written… worth reading and thinking about."* He did not numerically endorse 4/√φ as the exact value.

Independent of the FIGU record, **Harry Lear** arrived at π = 4/√φ through geometric proofs and CNC physical measurement, and **Christian Frehner's** Bulletin 123 (2019) reports physical measurements in the 3.144 range (a 1 m disc coming out ~3 mm over 3.141…, and an acquaintance measuring 3.1441).

## The honest boundary

- The contact reports support the **challenge**: π is erroneous, and exactness needs an instrumentarium Earth lacks.
- The **number** 4/√φ = 3.1446 comes from Harry Lear and the Kinash article (CR 856 / Sign of the Times 75), not from a direct Plejaren statement — and even that source flags the measurement as not yet precise enough to count as scientific fact.

Both columns belong on the table. The constructed world — where 4/√φ squares the circle exactly — and the measured analytic world — where the conventional series evaluate to 3.14159265… — are each real, and each keeps its own book.
