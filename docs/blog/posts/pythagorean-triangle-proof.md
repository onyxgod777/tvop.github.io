## ⚡ Why It Matters

A right triangle with sides **4**, **π**, and **16/π**
is the simplest possible Pythagorean test involving the circle constant. If π is truly transcendental
(3.1415926535...), then **4² + π² ≠ (16/π)²** — the triangle cannot close by
**0.0686**.

But if π = **4/√φ = √(√320−8) = 3.144605511...**, the equality holds **exactly**.
No approximation. No measurement error. The geometry proves itself.

*geometry* — the object casting the shadow. The ∼0.003 gap between them is the
**"extremely small error"** Ptaah described in Contact Report 712, and the first
five digits **3.1446** match what he confirmed in CR 856.
