**In every one of the five, the event described as news is the *effect*. The cause is upstream, human, and named: a decision to schedule a war's end to a vote; a decision to answer a blockade with strikes rather than a monitored port regime; a decision to announce deterrence as a date rather than a capability; a decision about how much margin to hold between a correct warning and an unheeded one; and a decision about how far a reckoning runs.**

In every case, the cause is **a human choice** — and therefore, the effect is **changeable**.
