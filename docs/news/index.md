The enclave's leader said **resources were being overwhelmed** after so many people entered by sea. The cause is a compound one: **a prolonged drought and record heatwave have lowered the Danube to levels that no longer provide sufficient cooling water**, and nuclear plants, which require vast volumes of cooling water, are among the first critical infrastructure to fail when river levels drop.
