### 🇮🇷 The Neighbours Did Not Close the Gates

???+ danger "Observed Cause"

## The Pattern

Look across these five — **a missile through a hospital's surgical ward that only the belligerents can describe**, **two envoys shuttling between capitals with no paper in hand**, **a land blockade refused by the three neighbours who would have to enforce it**, **a vice-president who resigned rather than have his school certificate examined**, and **a board meeting at midnight about the founder it has known since the 1990s** — and the structure of this week is not a decision. It is a **certification problem**.

**Every one of the five has collapsed into the same question: who is entitled to certify a fact, and what happens when the certifier is a party.** In Khan Younis the fact is whether a hospital was a command post; the only bodies offering a finding are the army that struck it and the authority that runs it, and the press that might have checked is barred from the Strip. In Moscow and Dublin the fact is whether the talks are progressing; the certification on offer is a plane landing and a handshake, because there is no text to read. In Baghdad, Ankara and Islamabad the fact is whether the blockade is being enforced; the states whose borders would have to close have declined the role, which leaves the measurement to the navy that can only see the sea. In Jakarta the fact was whether a vice-president met the qualification for his office; a court looked for the record and found none — after nineteen months, and the remedy chosen was a resignation rather than a verification. In Santa Clara the fact is whether a chief executive is fit to remain; the body that must certify it has eleven members, four of whom have known him since the 1990s, and no chair.

**The tell is what each of them is counting now, and what has stopped being counted.** Jerusalem counts signatures and dates — 67 on the speaker's request, 17 November at 4 p.m. — while an election result is disputed in the press and the security service's own allegations are leaked into the dispute. Moscow counts arrivals. Washington counts tonnes it cannot see crossing three borders it does not control, and designations it can publish instead. Jakarta counts sixty days and two names the president will supply. Nvidia counts dollars: eight per cent after hours, five hundred billion since Friday, four point two trillion in total — and it is the only one of the five where the number is published hourly and the judgement is still unavailable.

**What this does to causality is precise.** A fact that no neutral party can certify does not become false; it becomes *contestable*, and a contestable fact transfers power from the institution that would have judged it to whoever can afford the longest argument. That is why the cheapest intervention in all five files is the same one: a document that specifies the arbiter before the dispute arrives. A forensic protocol with ICRC access. An infrastructure annex with a named verification body. A published blockade instrument with an appeal mechanism. A candidate-verification standard applied before nomination. An investigation with an outside chair and a reporting date. §110's instruction is operational here in an unfamiliar way: the cause to be changed is not the strike, the siege, the resignation or the affair — it is the absence of a named certifier, which is a man-made omission and can be unmade by a signature. None of these five requires a party to lose. Each requires a party to accept an arbiter, which is the one concession that costs nothing and is therefore the one nobody offers.
