???+ danger "Observed Cause"
    Yesterday this page recorded a negotiation Brussels itself called the "last chance saloon." ??? warning "Projected Effect (if cause persists)"

    - **Weaponised interdependence cuts both ways, and Europe is the more exposed** — the direct effect of targeting Chinese supply chains is that Europe also depends heavily on Chinese critical raw materials, components and demand for its exports; the compound consequence is that a decisive European measure can be answered by a decisive Chinese shortage, so a policy meant to protect European industry can idle the very factories it aims to defend
