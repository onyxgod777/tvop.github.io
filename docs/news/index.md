> *"Prophecies serve as warnings for effects proceeding out of certain bad, evil causes set by you human beings of Earth… if you change the cause toward the good or positive, then the effect also changes. Every thing that exists becomes and passes — change is the only constant, and it begins with the causes you choose."*
>
> — *Goblet of the Truth*, §110–111, §240

The law of cause and effect is the most precise and impartial of all creational laws. It does not punish or reward — **it reflects**. Every thought, feeling, word, and deed generates a specific vibrational cause. That cause accumulates energy, compresses into lived experience, and returns — amplified — as your destiny.

On this page, we track **current world events as observed causes** and project their **necessary effects** if those causes persist unchanged. This is not fortune-telling. It is **causal inference** — the same principle that lets a physicist predict a trajectory from position and velocity. See the cause, know the effect. Change the cause, and the effect dissolves.

### 🇺🇦 Britain Delivers Long-Range Missile Blueprints to Kyiv on Ukraine's Independence Day — as Moscow Warns of "Consequences" and Tests a New Mobilization

???+ danger "Observed Cause"
    On August 24, 2026 — the 35th anniversary of Ukraine's independence — **UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham made his first foreign visit as premier to Kyiv, where he laid flowers at the National Military Memorial Cemetery, will hand over blueprints so Ukraine can build its own long-range missiles, and will chair his first meeting of the "coalition of the willing"** set up by the UK and France under his predecessor, Sir Keir Starmer (BBC, NYT). The centrepiece is a decision to let the defence firm MBDA declassify information on UK components for the long-range Scalp missile — the French equivalent of the Storm Shadow — so Ukraine can produce the weapon itself; Defence Minister Luke Pollard said the blueprints would help Ukraine "defend against this illegal invasion." European leaders joined Zelensky in Kyiv for the independence-day events, and Zelensky again pressed for 360 Patriot interceptors, warning that Ukraine's stockpiles are running low partly because of the war in Iran.

    The escalation is mutual. **Moscow warned of "consequences" over Kyiv's use of British drones in a stepped-up long-range campaign striking Russian oil refineries and warehouses — including those of the retailer Wildberries — while the WSJ reported Russia is testing readiness for a new round of military mobilization**, and Zelensky again rejected wartime elections as "a tsunami" for a country that martial law already holds without a vote (Time). The cause is now two-sided and industrial: **the West deepening the transfer of strike technology to out-produce Russia, and Russia answering with warnings, mobilization-readiness drills and an intensified assault on cities — each side betting that escalation and production, not negotiation, will break the other.**

    - **Mobilization-readiness and "consequences" warnings are the prelude to a harder Russian line** — the effect of Russia signalling a new call-up while threatening retaliation for British-supplied strikes is a renewed escalation cycle of deeper strikes and higher casualties, not a de-escalation
    - **The Patriot gap remains the war's soft underbelly** — with Kyiv's interceptors running low because production has been diverted to the Iran theatre, the effect of the missile-transfer focus is continued vulnerability to Russia's ballistic barrages, whatever the gains in long-range strike power
    - **The independence-day unity is also a political fault line** — the effect of European leaders rallying in Kyiv while Russia frames the West as the aggressor is a hardening of blocs, even as Zelensky's rejection of elections leaves Ukraine's own democratic question suspended for as long as the war lasts
    - **Yet escalation is a chosen cause on both sides** — the moment either capital decides that out-escalating the other is a losing bet and moves toward a negotiated settlement, the effect transforms from a lengthening, industrialised war into a managed end to it

    *"You have created vast changes in your world and in your humankind since time immemorial… although the majority of all these upheavals have been and are also in the present time of an extremely negative and evil nature, and indeed through your hatred that you bear towards your fellow human beings… through which you see yourselves incited to practice jealousy, take the first opportunity to wage wars and other terror…"* — Chapter 23, Verse 96

### 🤖 The Drone That Killed Three Was Guided Entirely by A.I. — Autonomous, Nvidia-Powered Weapons Cross a New Threshold in Ukraine

???+ danger "Observed Cause"
    On August 24, 2026, **the New York Times reported that a drone that killed three Ukrainians was "guided entirely by A.I." — an attack Ukrainian officials attributed to a Russian drone carrying an Nvidia chip, presaging a future of weaponry untethered to human control** (NYT). A companion report detailed that **some of Russia's A.I. drones are powered by Nvidia microcomputers: the tech giant says it does not sell the devices in Russia, yet they are widely available on resale markets and, when purchased that way, virtually impossible to track** — with Nvidia Jetson processors previously recovered from Russia's S-71 "Monochrome" cruise missile (NYT, Kyiv Post, Interesting Engineering).

    The cause is a **convergence of human-set choices: a war generating relentless demand for cheap, scalable killing; export-control regimes with porous resale markets that funnel Western microchips into Russia's weapons; and a weapons industry racing to automate the decision to kill**. What is new is the threshold crossed — a strike in which the targeting, and arguably the lethal decision, was made by an algorithm rather than a person in the loop, enabled by the very microcomputers built for civilian machines now repurposed as the brains of autonomous munitions.

??? warning "Projected Effect (if cause persists)"
    - **Autonomy removes the human from the decision to kill** — the direct effect of fully A.I.-guided munitions is that targeting, discrimination and the choice of whom to strike are made by software under battlefield conditions, faster and colder than any human judgment could be
    - **Export-control gaps make the escalation self-sustaining** — the effect of Western chips flowing through opaque resale markets is that Russia can keep automating its arsenal despite sanctions, and that every export-control regime becomes a contest between regulators and a grey market that is "virtually impossible to track"
    - **Threshold-crossing becomes contagious** — the effect of one side deploying autonomous killers is that the other follows suit, so a capability first used in Ukraine becomes the global norm, an arms race in which every belligerent races to remove its own humans from the lethal loop
    - **Accountability dissolves at the moment it is most needed** — the effect of algorithmic killing is a legal and moral vacuum: when an A.I. chooses its target, there is no operator to hold responsible, no intent to weigh, and the very structure of accountability on which the laws of war depend is hollowed out
    - **Yet the cause is a set of decisions, not an inevitability** — the moment nations choose to require human judgment in the lethal loop, to close the resale loopholes that feed the weapons, and to build guardrails on autonomous systems, the effect changes from a dehumanised arms race back to weapons under human control

    *"Truly, as far as technology is concerned, you human beings of Earth have made great progress; from the club to the atom bomb and from the wheelless cart to space rockets, you have pushed ahead with your progress, but in your inner nature, in your individuality, you have not developed greatly as an individuum, as a human being, but have negatively changed yourselves even further, by means of which you have created the entire unhonest structure of the society throughout the world."* — Chapter 23, Verse 87

    **The hinge:** The cause is the **choice to let technological capability outrun human judgment** — and the hinge is whether that choice is renewed or reversed. The verse is precise: humanity has progressed from the club to the atom bomb — and now to the autonomous drone — yet has not developed innerly, so the outer machinery is placed in the service of "the entire unhonest structure of the society." The currency's collapse has spread visible economic despair — Iranians queuing and hoarding, and the government signalling a fuel-price hike on the eve of the new US pressure (Al Jazeera). **Tehran answered with warnings and threats: officials warned any nation joining the US campaign would face consequences, national-security adviser Mohsen Rezaei vowed to prevent "a single drop of oil" from leaving the region, and Iran cautioned Gulf states not to sign on** (NYT, The Times of Israel). Meanwhile **fewer than 20 ships transited the Strait of Hormuz over the weekend** — a continued, tightly controlled squeeze (Reuters).

    The cause is the **cumulative force of a mutual economic siege now visibly landing on ordinary people**: Washington's escalating dollar-based pressure ("economic D-Day") squeezing Iran's exports and reserves, and Tehran's answer of endurance, counter-threats and the Hormuz lever. ### 🌍 El Niño Dries Indonesia's Peatlands and Wildfires Explode Across Borneo — a Climate Pattern Amplified by Human Hands

???+ danger "Observed Cause"
    On August 24, 2026, **the New York Times reported that as El Niño dries Indonesia, wildfires are intensifying, with dried-out peatlands — a kind of wetland that can smoulder for months — behind some of the country's worst fire seasons, and this year could be one of them** (NYT). The fires are concentrated in Borneo, **Indonesia has been bolstering troop numbers to combat them (Al Jazeera), police arrested 72 people suspected of starting forest fires (AP), and the haze is spreading to neighbouring Malaysia (ABC)**. Analysts note El Niño is not the sole culprit: decades of drained, degraded peatland and land-clearing fires have made the landscape far more flammable than any natural dry spell alone would (The Conversation).

    The cause is a **convergence of a natural climate pattern with human-set causes: El Niño's dry spell igniting a landscape that human activity has primed to burn — peatlands drained for agriculture, forests cleared, and fires set to clear land — all layered on emissions-driven warming that intensifies the drought**. The blast of fires sweeping Borneo is thus not simply weather; it is weather meeting a terrain that human choices have turned into tinder.

??? warning "Projected Effect (if cause persists)"
    - **A health and displacement crisis across the region** — the direct effect of explosive peatland fires is a choking haze that blankets Indonesia and drifts into Malaysia and Singapore, sickening millions and grounding flights, while villages in the fire zones face displacement and loss
    - **Peat fires become a carbon bomb** — the effect of burning peatlands — among the world's densest carbon stores — is a massive release of greenhouse gases that accelerates the very warming that dries the land further, a feedback loop in which today's fires worsen tomorrow's droughts
    - **Ecological destruction compounds** — the effect of fires sweeping Borneo's forests is the loss of habitat for species found nowhere else on Earth, an irreversible erosion of biodiversity that follows the pattern of extinctions the teaching names among humanity's gravest acts
    - **The response strains the state** — the effect of deploying troops to fight fires and arresting suspected arsonists is a diversion of resources and a test of whether deterrence can stop a practice driven by the economics of land clearing
    - **Yet the human causes are all changeable** — the moment Indonesia and its neighbours end land-clearing fires, restore drained peatlands and cut the emissions that amplify the drought, the effect transforms from a catastrophic fire season back toward a manageable dry spell

    *"How you look back on these old times today, which according to your present cognitions and terms were bearing in them blatant misery and boundless unright… is how your descendants will one day look back on the past, if they think about your time, your actions which are far from the creational laws, your unintellect and your irrationality, your contempt for humankind, the destruction of the nature, the eradication of many plants, fishes, birds, animals and other creatures and the destruction of the climate."* — Chapter 28, Verse 379

    **The hinge:** The cause is not El Niño alone but the **human acts that turned a natural dry spell into a catastrophe** — the drained peatlands, the land-clearing fires, the emissions that deepen the drought — and the hinge is whether humanity changes those acts. The verse holds a mirror to the present: our descendants will look back on our time as we look back on eras of "blatant misery," and what they will record is the destruction of nature, the eradication of species and the destruction of the climate. None of that is fate; each is the accumulated effect of causes we still hold the power to change. Ending land-clearing fires, restoring the peatlands, and curbing the emissions that amplify the dry season are all free choices available now. Whether this El Niño is remembered as a disaster or as the moment the causes were changed is being decided in the fields, the legislature and the policies of every nation that feeds the warming — not in the weather.

Though they span the West deepening Ukraine's strike capability with long-range missile blueprints as Russia warns of "consequences" and tests a new mobilization, the crossing of a new threshold as an A.I.-guided, Nvidia-powered drone kills three Ukrainians without a human in the loop, the collapse of Iran's rial past two million under Washington's "economic D-Day" while Tehran vows to hit back, El Niño igniting Indonesia's dried peatlands into a firestorm that human land-clearing and emissions have primed to burn, and Israel answering Gaza's kite launches with lethal strikes even as a war-crimes probe and the cooling Syria talks hang overhead — they reveal a single causal architecture: **escalation, coercion and retribution as the default instruments of power, and technological and military progress racing far ahead of the inner development of those who wield it**.
