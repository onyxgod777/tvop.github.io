Every thought, feeling, word, and deed generates a specific vibrational cause. That cause accumulates energy, compresses into lived experience, and returns — amplified — as your destiny.

On this page, we track **current world events as observed causes** and project their **necessary effects** if those causes persist unchanged. This is not fortune-telling. ### 🇺🇳 The Criteria Arrive With the Invoice

???+ danger "Observed Cause"
    **The tariff list forecast here on Tuesday was published on Thursday, and it came with the one thing the previous round never had: a document per country.** The **US Trade Representative's office** announced proposed duties on **fourteen jurisdictions** — **China** at up to **40 per cent**, **Vietnam** up to **35 per cent**, and **the European Union, Singapore, Switzerland, Indonesia, Malaysia, Cambodia, Thailand, Korea, Taiwan, Bangladesh, Japan and India** facing either **20 or 25 per cent** — alongside, in the BBC's account, *"extensive reports of how each jurisdiction's trade practices purportedly impact American commerce."* USTR **Jamieson Greer** said the levies would *"level the playing field for American workers and chart a new, more sustainable course for the world economy,"* and that partners had for *"decades"* run industrial strategies including *"targeting manufacturing, wage and labor suppression"* and *"other market distorting practices that tilt the trade balance in their favour while eroding America's manufacturing base."* Three structural differences from the April 2025 round: the new duties **do not take effect until next year** and must pass **public consultation and potentially hearings**; the legal basis is **Section 301** — the authority to retaliate against specific foreign trade practices — rather than the emergency presidential powers under which the earlier tariffs were **struck down**; and the rates are **capped in practice** by ceilings Trump has already agreed with some partners, which is why listed rates and applied rates will diverge. Economists have warned the taxes can fuel inflation, and the announcement follows a poor Republican midterm result.

??? warning "Projected Effect (if cause persists)"
    - **A rate attached to a named practice is a refutable claim, and refutability is the whole difference** — fourteen published reports, each asserting a specific distortion, each open to consultation; the direct effect is that a government can now answer the finding rather than plead for a favour; the compound consequence is that the schedule becomes arguable in a way a loyalty list would not have been, which means the tariff's durability now rests on whether the reports survive cross-examination — and a report written to justify a rate is not written to survive it
    - **Section 301 buys legality by narrowing the instrument, and narrow instruments are the ones that get litigated** — retaliation against a found practice, not a blanket emergency duty; the direct effect is that the earlier legal defeat is unlikely to repeat in the same form; the compound consequence is that the findings themselves become the target, so every rate now depends on an evidentiary file that a court, a successor administration or a trading partner's counter-report can dismantle — the tariff is as strong as its weakest paragraph
    - **Deferring effect to next year converts an announcement into a negotiation with a published agenda** — consultation, possible hearings, caps that already reduce some rates; the direct effect is a window in which the schedule can still move; the compound consequence is that the window is the leverage: the same fourteen governments will spend the next months arguing about findings rather than building plants, and the investment the policy is meant to attract waits for the outcome of the argument it just created
    - **A schedule differentiated by measured grievance still differentiates by relationship at the top of the range** — 40 against China, 35 against Vietnam, 20 or 25 for the rest, with caps negotiated bilaterally; the direct effect is that the ranking is now defensible in a report; the compound consequence is that the ranking is also a map of where production will move next, so the fourth country down the list receives the factories, the fifteenth receives nothing, and the distortion is relocated rather than removed
    - **The consumer's clock and the factory's clock still run in opposite directions, and this round lengthens the gap** — duties next year after consultation, price effects within a shipping cycle of implementation, plants in years; the direct effect is that the political cost arrives on schedule and the political benefit does not; the compound consequence is that a policy judged before its own effects exist will be revised by whichever government inherits the invoice

    *"Anyone who undertakes haggling (usury/greed) in any form of commerce and unrightfully demands a markup (interest or price increase) shall be exposed to the punishment of recompense through the authority or jurisdiction, so that the disadvantaged ones do not suffer any loss of goods and chattels, possessions and fortune; in commerce involving goods and things, the markup (interest or price increase) shall be regulated and defined by the authority according to the goods or things and their value…"* — *Goblet of the Truth*, Chapter 2, Verse 388

    **The hinge:** §2:388 is unusually specific for a text of this age: the markup *"shall be regulated and defined by the authority according to the goods or things and their value."* The measure is the **value of the thing**, and the authority is granted the power to define it — which is exactly the machinery the USTR has now built. But the verse names the criterion: **the goods and their value**, not the conduct of the government that made them. A tariff justified by a country's industrial policy is a judgement about a government; a tariff justified by a dumped price is a judgement about a price. The published reports are the first serious attempt to make the second kind of case — and the test is whether a partner that rebuts a finding can get a rate changed, because a finding that cannot be rebutted is a verdict. **First,** publish the **methodology** behind each finding — the counterfactual, the data, the margin — so that the number can be checked rather than merely read. **Second,** state in advance what evidence would **lower** a rate, because a consultation in which no outcome but the announced one is possible is a hearing, not a process. **Third,** keep **critical minerals** out of the retaliation schedule entirely: gallium, germanium and antimony are inputs to every supply chain on earth, including the American one the tariff is meant to protect.

### 🔥 Twenty-Four Hundred Kilometres

???+ danger "Observed Cause"

On Tuesday the **Libyan National Army** — the force that controls eastern and southern Libya, maintains close relations with Russia, and fields its own navy and air force — named its dead as **Al-Taher Abdullah al-Sadiq** and **Lieutenant Ali Mohammed al-Said**, said they had been wounded in what it called *"the treacherous Ukrainian aggression against the port of Benghazi,"* and **vowed retaliation at a time and place of its choosing**.     - **The port is the real target of the precedent, not the corvette** — Benghazi is where Russia refuels, resupplies and moves cargo into Africa; a drone that can reach a berthed warship 2,400 km away can reach the tanker, the terminal and the cargo manifest; the direct effect is that Russian naval logistics in the Mediterranean now require escort and dispersion; the compound consequence is that insurance, crewing and port fees price the new risk before any navy does, and the Russian presence in Libya becomes more expensive to sustain than the base is worth — which is the outcome Kyiv wants and the one that will spread the war into a country trying to reunify

    - **The dead are the part that cannot be answered with a statement, and the LNA knows it** — two named officers, funerals, a force with a navy that has just watched a corvette sink in its own harbour; the direct effect is domestic pressure on a command that already governs by force; the compound consequence is that the cheapest way for the LNA to demonstrate capability is against something soft and Ukrainian in the west, which is precisely the escalation that pulls Tripoli's militias in and turns a proxy arrangement into a Libyan civil-war phase

### ⛓️ The Mercedes Defence

???+ danger "Observed Cause"
    **A 35-year-old boat dealer near Dortmund is suspected by European investigators of being a principal supplier to the gangs that run the English Channel, and the numbers in the file are not marginal.** According to documents reviewed by the **BBC** with **WDR**, **NDR**, **Süddeutsche Zeitung** and **Le Monde**, nearly **a third** of small-boat crossings in the **first four months of 2026** allegedly involved boats or engines from **MaReBoote**, the firm run by managing director **Marvin Reuter**. French investigators say at least **82 boats** from the firm reached the English coast in just over two years to May, and press photographs going back five years show the company's logo on dinghies crowded with migrants. His equipment is suspected of being linked to **two fatal crossings in which four men died**. Reuter is **contesting extradition to France**, where he could face charges of **smuggling, criminal conspiracy and involuntary manslaughter**; he cannot be formally charged until extradited, is presumed innocent, declined to answer the BBC's questions at his business and said he was reporting the reporters to the police. The evidence base includes confessions of alleged accomplices and covert recordings made in a jail cell of an alleged gang leader. In **2021** Reuter told the *Sun* and the *Guardian* he felt *"frustrated"* and *"anxious"* that his vessels had ended up with smugglers; at the time a German detective offered the analogy that if somebody buys a Mercedes and does something wrong in another country, the seller cannot help that. The disclosures land in the same week that **UK net migration fell to its lowest level since 1997** outside the pandemic, driven largely by fewer arrivals from outside Europe — the result, the New York Times reports, of deliberate policy after public backlash.

??? warning "Projected Effect (if cause persists)"
    - **The bottleneck is a shop, not a beach, and identifying it changes the entire enforcement model** — one firm's hulls and outboards in a third of early-2026 crossings; the direct effect is that disruption can be aimed upstream, at catalogues, invoices and export licences; the compound consequence is that the market adapts the way all markets do — the next supplier is smaller, less traceable, and sells through three intermediaries — so the win is real but temporary unless the instrument becomes a licensing regime rather than a prosecution
    - **The "Mercedes defence" is a claim that a product's destination is unknowable, and the file is an argument that it was knowable** — 82 boats in two years, logo-bearing dinghies in press photographs for five years, a warning raised by the seller himself in 2021; the direct effect is that the unknowability claim is now contested on the record; the compound consequence is that the standard for component sellers shifts from intent to **pattern** — and a pattern standard, applied to outboard engines, inflatable hulls and life jackets, reaches a great many legitimate businesses that will now have to document their customers
    - **Extradition contested is a case that will be litigated for years while the crossings continue this winter** — French charges unavailable until surrender, a defendant presumed innocent, a German privacy regime that blurs his face; the direct effect is that no finding exists while the activity alleged is ongoing; the compound consequence is that deterrence is deferred to a courtroom calendar, and operators learn the useful lesson — that the cost of being caught is measured in years of procedure, not in a closed workshop
    - **A smuggling economy needs boats, engines and fuel, and only the first is being chased** — MaReBoote's outboards named in the file, French investigators tracing hulls; the direct effect is pressure on one input; the compound consequence is that the engine is the durable, serial-numbered, resaleable component and therefore the most traceable of the three, which makes a registration requirement on high-thrust outboards the cheapest effective intervention available — cheaper than a prosecution, faster than a treaty, and it does not require proving anyone's state of mind
    - **Falling net migration and a thriving smuggling supply chain are not a contradiction, they are the same policy seen from two ends** — arrivals down to a 1997 low on legal routes, a criminal market pricing the residual demand; the direct effect is that the political metric improves while the dangerous route persists; the compound consequence is that the people who can no longer come legally are precisely those who will pay a smuggler, so restricting the legal channel raises the value of the illegal one — and the dinghy is where that value is collected

    *"…as slavery as well as other trafficking in people of your kind (human beings) and servitude as well as bondage contravene all rights and are reprehensible as well as disallowed (forbidden); you shall turn to people of your kind (fellow human beings) in kind-heartedness and love and not harm them in any wise."* — *Goblet of the Truth*, Chapter 4, Verse 51

    **The hinge:** §4:51 does not legislate about boats. It names the harm — trafficking in human beings — and then states the duty in the widest possible form: *"not harm them in any wise."* That phrasing is what dissolves the Mercedes analogy, because the analogy works only if the harm is confined to the buyer's use of the product. The verse's standard is the harm done to the person in the dinghy, and it asks whether the seller's arrangement was compatible with it. A dealer who sold eighty-two hulls into a channel with one use, and said in 2021 that he was anxious about it, was on his own account in a position to know. **First,** make **outboard engines registrable** by serial number at point of sale in the EU and UK, because an engine is the one component that cannot be improvised and can be traced. **Second,** require **end-use declarations** on high-capacity inflatables, with a licensing condition rather than a criminal one — administrative, fast, and it does not require a jury to infer intent. **Third,** fund the **French file to conclusion** in Reuter's own jurisdiction, because an extradition fight that outlasts the news cycle teaches the next supplier that the only real risk is publicity.

### ☣️ Zero Detected

???+ danger "Observed Cause"
    **The Army detonated the barrels at nine o'clock on Wednesday morning, and by the afternoon its monitors were reporting nothing.** Sixty-eight drums — roughly **five tonnes, some 10,000 pounds** — of deteriorating **lead azide** were destroyed at the **Iowa Army Ammunition Plant** in Middletown, the day before Thanksgiving, in the blast this page described on Tuesday as the week's clearest case of a warning that arrived after its method had been chosen. The outcome, on the Army's own instruments, was better than its critics expected. **General Eric Shirley**, commander of Army Sustainment Command, called the operation *"tremendously successful"* and said it had *"wildly exceeded"* expectations, with *"no infrastructure damage in or around our installation."* Acting Secretary of the Army **Adam R. Telle** wrote that the detonation was a *"HUGE success"* and that *"the threat of an uncontrolled spontaneous detonation has been eliminated."* The **Environmental Protection Agency** reported that its real-time air monitors recorded **"ZERO"** detection of airborne lead, consistent before, during and after the blast, and said it would remain on site *"for as long as necessary AFTER detonation."* The shelter-in-place order was lifted. What has not been answered is the question the critics actually raised: **Illinois' EPA** had warned the blast would produce a *"dust plume containing lead particles"* that could travel beyond the plant and **deposit on surrounding surfaces** — and air monitoring measures the air. The blast also leaves the plant's future in limbo, with earlier contamination cleanup potentially undone by the detonation itself.

??? warning "Projected Effect (if cause persists)"
    - **A successful blast is not the same finding as a safe one, and the difference is the difference between a monitor and a soil sample** — zero airborne lead at the fence line, a plume model predicting deposition, pasture downwind; the direct effect is that the acute question — did anyone breathe it — is answered well; the compound consequence is that the chronic question — what is now on the ground — has no instrument attached to it, and an answer given only for the medium that was measured will be read as an answer for the medium that was not
    - **"Wildly exceeded expectations" is a statement about a model, and the model was the thing under suspicion** — a predicted plume that did not materialise, credited to mitigation; the direct effect is that the mitigation is vindicated where it was tested; the compound consequence is that the expectation was itself the product of the agencies that chose the method, so the vindication is real but not independent — and an outcome certified by its author is evidence, not proof, which is why the third-party soil data now matters more than the press conference
    - **The absence of a pre-blast baseline is the one failure that this success cannot repair** — no published soil and water data for the affected counties before Wednesday; the direct effect is that any lead found next spring cannot be attributed with confidence to this blast or to seventy years of the plant; the compound consequence is that the burden of proof inverts — the household must show the contamination came from the detonation and cannot, so a genuine harm and a spurious claim become indistinguishable and both go uncompensated
    - **Removing ten thousand pounds of degraded detonator does not remove the reason it was there** — a plant holding deteriorating explosives in the ground, a disposal decision deferred until falling temperatures forced it, a community notified on 17 November for a blast planned three days later; the direct effect is that the acute hazard is gone; the compound consequence is that the underlying practice — storage without a published disposal schedule — remains, so the next stockpile will also be discovered by deadline, and a system that resolves hazards by emergency will keep producing emergencies it can then call successes
    - **A clean result handled as a public-relations event spends the credibility the next emergency will need** — a governor posting photographs, an acting secretary posting in capitals, a president calling it a success before the monitors had finished a day; the direct effect is that confidence rises; the compound consequence is that the same officials have now staked their word on an outcome whose long-tail data does not exist yet, and if deposition is found in the spring the correction will cost more trust than the celebration bought

    *"Do not wait for the warnings of the true prophets to be fulfilled… because on the day when the warning call of the prophets comes about it will be too late… and truly, when the predicted events come to pass then you cannot turn back (wind back) time in order to undo everything…"* — *Goblet of the Truth*, Chapter 7, Verse 54

    **The hinge:** §7:54 is about the irreversibility of a fulfilled warning, and Wednesday's blast is irreversible in the precise sense the verse means: whatever is on that ground now cannot be un-detonated. The Army's case was that the alternative — an uncontrolled detonation on its own schedule — was worse, and on the evidence so far the Army was right, which is why the hinge is not about stopping the blast. It is about the *next* one. The warning that was available and unused was not a prophecy; it was baseline data — cheap, unglamorous, and requiring collection before nine o'clock on Wednesday. **First,** fund **independent soil and pasture testing** across the plume path at public expense, with results published, because a farmer who must pay to prove her land is clean has been fined for someone else's decision. **Second,** require a **published disposal schedule** for every stockpile of deteriorating energetic material on US soil — a date, not a contingency — so the next detonation is planned rather than precipitated. **Third,** write the **notification standard** into the record: Fort Madison learned on 17 November about a blast planned for the 20th, and no future community should learn of a five-tonne detonation from a leaflet delivered after the decision.

### 🇮🇱 Considerable Difficulties

???+ danger "Observed Cause"
    **The judges trying Israel's prime minister told the prosecution, in open court, that its most serious case was unlikely to succeed.** At a hearing in Jerusalem on **Wednesday**, the panel in **Benjamin Netanyahu's** long-running corruption trial recommended **dropping Case 4000** — the bribery, fraud and breach of trust case centred on favourable coverage from the Walla news site in exchange for regulatory benefits to the Bezeq telecoms owner — recommended **reconsidering a second case**, and suggested resolving the **third through an agreement**. The presiding judge, **Rivka Friedman-Feldman**, told **Attorney General Gali Baharav-Miara** that proving the bribery charge faced *"considerable difficulties"*, and indicated the lesser counts of fraud and breach of trust would also be problematic because they too rested on establishing that Netanyahu had **given instructions** favouring **Shaul Elovitch**. ??? warning "Projected Effect (if cause persists)"
    - **A bench that names the weakness of a case has made a finding about proof, and the public will hear it as a finding about conduct** — *"considerable difficulties"* in proving bribery; the direct effect is that an acquittal or a deal is now the expected outcome; the compound consequence is that the distinction between "not proven" and "not done" collapses in a political system that has spent years arguing about exactly this, and the collapse is not the judges' fault — it is what any verdict does when a trial is treated as a referendum

    - **Two clocks are now running, and the legal one is being asked to finish before the political one starts** — a caretaker premier, a bench inviting a quick close, an opposition with a 6 December deadline to assemble a majority; the direct effect is that the case's disposition becomes an input to coalition arithmetic; the compound consequence is that whichever clock finishes first will be accused of timing the other, so the outcome — whatever it is — arrives with its legitimacy already discounted, and the discount is paid by the institution that moved second
    - **The charges that survive are the ones about instructions, and instructions are the part that can be documented** — fraud and breach of trust resting on whether Netanyahu gave directions favouring Elovitch; the direct effect is that the remaining case turns on a narrower, more provable question; the compound consequence is that the public argument migrates to that question with none of the previous case's clarity, so a country that has organised its politics around one trial will find itself arguing about a technical element of a lesser count for another two years
    - **A caretaker government that may be replaced within ten days is negotiating the country's wars** — Gaza strikes that killed a Hamas political official and his wife among eight Palestinians this week, a northern front, an Iranian file; the direct effect is that decisions are being taken by an authority whose tenure is contingent; the compound consequence is that adversaries price the transition, allies defer commitments, and the interval between a legal reprieve and a coalition is exactly the window in which an irreversible decision is cheapest to take and hardest to attribute

    *"And if you have to decide (determine) between people of your kind (human beings) in any things, then only decide according to equitableness (fairness) and according to the circumstances, but not however according to belief (assumptions) and apparent evidence (indications), so that you do not accuse unfairly but only reach a judgement in rightness according to the laws of the primal power (Creation) and prescribe a punishment in a commensurate wise…"* — *Goblet of the Truth*, Chapter 4, Verse 82

    **The hinge:** §4:82 asks a judge for two things that pull in opposite directions — decide by **the circumstances**, but *not* by **apparent evidence**, so that no one is accused unfairly. What the verse also forbids is the *shortcut*: an agreement that ends the matter without reasons leaves no judgement in rightness, only an outcome. **First,** if there is to be an **agreement**, publish its terms and its findings, because a settlement that names no facts settles nothing. **Second,** let the **remaining count** be tried on its documents rather than folded into the deal, since the question of instructions is precisely the question a court can answer. **Third,** separate the **6 December coalition deadline** from the case in public statements by both sides, because the one thing that would make a lawful outcome look lawless is a calendar that appears to have been shared.

## The Pattern

Read the five together — **a tariff schedule justified country by country**, **a corvette sunk 2,400 kilometres from the front**, **a workshop that supplied a third of the boats**, **a detonation that turned out cleaner than predicted**, **a prosecution advised to fold** — and the common structure is the one this page identified yesterday, arriving at its next stage. Yesterday the decision had been moved to a place where scrutiny could not follow it. **This week the paperwork came back.**

**The records exist, and they are public.** The USTR published fourteen jurisdictional findings instead of a price list. The Libyan National Army published the names of its two dead officers and the words of its reprisal. A European investigation put a company name, a managing director, eighty-two hulls and two fatal crossings into a file that five news organisations could read. The EPA published monitor readings of zero. A bench stated, in open court and on the record, that the prosecution's principal case faced *"considerable difficulties."* None of this was transparency offered as a gift. It is what happens when a decision is too large to be taken quietly.

**And the new defence is thinner than the old one: it is procedure.** A tariff is defensible because the finding exists, and the finding is defensible because it was written by the office that wanted the rate. A reprisal is deferred because a pledge needs a time and a place, and the choosing takes as long as the politics requires. A prosecution waits on an extradition treaty, then on an agreement, then on a coalition deadline. A blast is certified by the agency that modelled it. A verdict becomes a settlement. In each case the instrument is now visible — which means the argument has moved from *who decided* to *whether the record is independent*, and that is a better argument to have.

**And the cost still lands where the decision is not.** The importer who pays the rate and the consumer who pays the importer. The Ukrainian operator in western Libya whose host's hospitality is now a liability, and the Libyan lieutenant's family in Benghazi. The man in the dinghy whose crossing was arranged by a purchase order in North Rhine-Westphalia. The farmer downwind in southeast Iowa who will be asked to prove, in the spring, what her soil held on Wednesday morning. The Israeli voter who will be told that a case has ended and will not be told what it found. None of them appear in the reports that now exist about them.
