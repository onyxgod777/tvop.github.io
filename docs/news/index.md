## The Pattern

Look across these five stories. Though they span **53 killed at a police training camp in Shabwa as Houthi missiles reach an Aramco office tower and attacks on shipping at Hormuz hit a record week**; **a Zelensky adviser telling the BBC that Ukrainians have died because US warnings arrive a minute before the missile**; **a million wildfire hotspots in Indonesia and 1.5 tonnes of dry ice over Palembang while the haze chokes three countries**; **191 murders — all but one of them children — proved against a Kenyan preacher and 28 of his leaders**; and **a Japanese spacecraft launched to bring 10 grams of Phobos home** — the common structure is unmistakable.

**In every one of the five, the event described as news is the *effect*. The cause is upstream, human and named: a decision, by a coalition and its adversaries, to answer a strike with a strike and leave the political objective unpublished; a decision, by a patron, to hold a warning feed as a lever in a negotiation about oil; a decision, by a plantation sector and the state that licenses it, that burning is cheaper than clearing; a decision, by a preacher, that a child's hunger was a sacrament — and, in the fifth, a decision to spend nine years and a launch to answer a question honestly.**

Four of these five are causes that were *chosen and are being repeated*: the missile, the throttle, the burn, the fast.
