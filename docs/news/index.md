???+ danger "Observed Cause"

### 🤖 Forty Technologies and a Draft — Beijing Puts Its Best Models Behind an Export Gate

???+ danger "Observed Cause"
    A new entry, and the cause is a document rather than an event: a draft catalogue that turns an open model into an instrument of state. **China's commerce and science ministries have released a draft update to the national catalogue of technologies whose export is banned or restricted, adding around 40 technologies across artificial intelligence, biomedicine and solar photovoltaics, of which 23 are AI technologies covering large models, AI agents, compute clusters and data processing; the draft is out for public comment** (The Decoder; Global Times; FT; Reuters). **The AI controls bite in two cases. First, models trained on "important data" as defined under China's Data Security Law and used to provide services on the basis of it — lawful public data exempt, with a tolerance of up to 0.1% where the data was cleaned, never appears in outputs and does not materially boost core-technology capability. Second, the core technologies of a large model — weights, checkpoints, source code, architecture, training and inference methods — once it reaches 10^26 FLOPs of training compute, at least 2 trillion parameters, or a global top-ten ranking as determined by public evaluators and industry platforms** (The Decoder). **AI that can autonomously write or alter malware to attack critical infrastructure, the military or government agencies faces a total export ban; tools that find software vulnerabilities face new limits** (The Decoder). **The draft deliberately protects China's open-weight boom: publishing restricted technology as open source qualifies for "automatic licensing", as does running one's own data centres abroad, taping out chips overseas or working with foreign partners; using restricted models through API calls, apps, websites or SaaS stays outside the controls, while installing them on servers abroad requires a licence — and until the final rules take effect, companies must register affected exports through the commerce ministry's portal** (The Decoder). **In the same week, the US Department of Homeland Security ordered all DeepSeek technology removed from civilian government agencies and technology built with its models banned — an order made public on Monday but effective since the end of July, covering Treasury, State and Justice while excluding the Department of Defense; the White House's technology lead, Michael Kratsios, had written in an internal memo that "malicious exploitation" of American AI firms was occurring, mainly by rivals in China, and both Anthropic and OpenAI have claimed their models were essentially copied** (BBC).

    **The two measures are the same measure read from opposite ends, and the symmetry is the story.** **Washington restricts what Chinese models may be *used* inside its government; Beijing restricts what Chinese models may be *taken* out of the country — and both are written as national security while functioning as industrial policy. Beijing's thresholds are a definition of strategic value: two trillion parameters, 10^26 FLOPs, a top-ten ranking. Everything below the line may travel freely; everything above it needs a licence — or a publication, because releasing the weights as open source is the one route that clears automatically** (The Decoder). **That exemption is the tell. A state that wanted to stop proliferation would ban publication; a state that wants to keep its open-weight ecosystem ahead while retaining the ability to close the gate later fast-tracks it — which is what a strategist quoted by The Decoder described as "establishing the optionality to shut down future open-weight models at will", noting that the framework is a "mirror image" of the weight controls Washington has already imposed** (The Decoder).

Whether Britain's North Sea is managed down with a plan, or drained until the market closes it, is not fixed by the geology; it is a decision held by named ministers, a named regulator and named companies, and one that a different choice could still make.

## The Pattern

Look across these five stories. Though they span **73 security recruits killed at a camp in Shabwa and five ballistic missiles at the presidential palace in Aden, with British Typhoons dispatched to defend Saudi cities and Taiz besieged**; **a curfew in the centre of Paris after 550,000 demonstrators, more than 2,500 arrests and 1,000 injured police, with France unable to agree on who lit the fire**; **6,509 dead in Venezuela's June earthquakes against a newspaper's finding that the true figure is probably higher, and a reconstruction that blames structural flaws**; **a Chinese draft catalogue putting 23 AI technologies and every model above 2 trillion parameters behind an export gate, answered by a US ban on DeepSeek in its own agencies**; and **Britain approving the Jackdaw gas field while deferring Rosebank and the climate question** — the common structure is unmistakable.

**In every one of the five, the event described as news is the *effect*. The cause is upstream, human and named: a decision, by two governments and a militia, to answer a strike with a strike and never publish the political objective; a decision, by a state, that a curfew is cheaper than a school budget; a decision, by engineers and the offices that approve them, to build for one shock instead of two; a decision, by two ministries, that a model's weights are a national asset to be licensed; and a decision, by a government, that a field is a smaller unit of accounting than a basin.**

Three of these five were taken deliberately this week by people who knew what they were choosing: the draft catalogue, the drilling approval, the curfew. One is the accumulated effect of decisions taken years ago and only now being counted: the Venezuelan towers. And one is a war in which every party is answering the last strike — the only one of the five where the cause is being actively renewed while it is being described.

In every case, the human cause is **a choice** — and therefore, the effect is **changeable**. Two of these five moved within twenty-four hours: the Yemen toll rose from 53 to 73 and a palace was struck, and more than 2,500 people were arrested in France. The other three are slower and therefore harder: a code revision takes a parliament, an export catalogue takes a final rule, and a gas field takes months to tie in — which is precisely why the slowest causes are the ones least likely to be changed by anything except a decision taken early.

**Yesterday's Ukraine entry moved as well, and in the direction this framework expects.** The Kyiv adviser who told the BBC that warning data arrived "one minute before the attack" has since been answered by President Zelensky saying intelligence sharing "works now", as a US envoy prepared to meet President Putin in Russia and an American prisoner was released. The throttled feed was a cause; it was named, it was contested, and within days it changed — not because anyone was persuaded, but because the cost of the arrangement became public.
