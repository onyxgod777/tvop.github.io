The law of cause and effect is the most precise and impartial of all creational laws. It does not punish or reward — **it reflects**. Every thought, feeling, word, and deed generates a specific vibrational cause. That cause accumulates energy, compresses into lived experience, and returns — amplified — as your destiny.

On this page, we track **current world events as observed causes** and project their **necessary effects** if those causes persist unchanged. This is not fortune-telling. It is **causal inference** — the same principle that lets a physicist predict a trajectory from position and velocity. See the cause, know the effect. Change the cause, and the effect dissolves.

### 🌍 The Hundredth Parade

???+ danger "Observed Cause"

### 🇪🇺 Twenty-Seven, Not Twenty-Four

???+ danger "Observed Cause"

    - **A budget negotiated in the year before an election is negotiated by people who are campaigning** — France votes in 2027, Italy and others with it; the direct effect is that no government can concede a rebate or a cut in public; the compound consequence is that deferral pushes the decision into the very election year the deadline was designed to precede, so the deal gets worse the later it gets, and the arc of the negotiation now points toward a short extension and a series of ad hoc instruments instead of a framework

    - **A housing crisis that produces a general strike and a snap election will produce the same elsewhere, and the EU has no housing competence** — four million on strike on 11 November, the largest mobilisation since 2012, rents rising across the eurozone's large cities; the direct effect is that national governments legislate on rents without controlling interest rates, construction costs or cross-border capital; the compound consequence is that the instrument that would move the number fastest is monetary and the instrument that is available is municipal, so the politics of housing is a politics of visible gestures — and visible gestures are what far-right parties sell best
    - **Thirty-nine million voters, and the government will be decided by a handful of regional seats** — Junts in Catalonia, the PNV in the Basque Country, a Senate that is also being elected; the direct effect is that the price of those seats is fiscal autonomy and transferred competences; the compound consequence is that the money any government needs for housing is the money it must first hand to the regions whose support it needs, so the coalition arithmetic and the housing policy compete for the same euros — and the region with the most leverage is the one with the least housing shortage

    *"Alms and good gifts for the poor and needy are all-timely for those who are defined as (verifiably) living in poverty and need, and those who are incapable of earning their bread themselves; alms and good gifts are also for the old who are infirm and can no longer do anything of importance (work); the unknowing ones amongst you believe that those defined as poor and needy as well as the old and infirm are free from need if they refrain from demanding (begging); you may recognise the truly honest poor and needy and the aged and infirm by their appearance (conduct), because they do not ask importunately and they direct their eyes to the ground in humiliation (shamefacedness)."* — *Goblet of the Truth*, Chapter 2, Verse 385

    **The hinge:** §2:385 is unusually specific about who is owed help: those *"verifiably living in poverty and need,"* those *"incapable of earning their bread themselves,"* and *"the old who are infirm and can no longer do anything of importance."* It then makes a claim about visibility that lands directly on this election: the ones in need *"do not ask importunately and they direct their eyes to the ground in shamefacedness"* — which is to say, need is systematically undercounted by any procedure that waits for a demand. A housing market allocates by willingness and ability to pay, and an 87-year-old pensioner is neither. **First,** legislate at the **point of eviction** rather than at the point of price: a court-supervised pause and rehousing duty for tenants who are aged, infirm or caring for dependants is a narrow, cheap, fast instrument that no constitutional court has yet struck down, and it addresses the exact event that triggered this election. **Second,** **count the stock** — municipal registers of empty homes and short-let licences, published quarterly — because 700,000 is an estimate, and a shortage that cannot be measured cannot be legislated against. **Third,** require every coalition agreement to be **published in full before the investiture vote**, including the housing chapter and the regional transfers, because a government assembled from regional vetoes will spend its housing money on the price of those vetoes, and the voter who cast a ballot about rent is entitled to see the line where it went.

## The Pattern

Read the five together — **an aggressor described as a friend**, **two watchmen described as operatives**, **a storm described as nature**, **an objection described as consensus**, **a rent described as the market** — and the common structure is **the word that decides the duty.** Each harm today was routed through a designation, and the designation settled the obligation before anyone examined the facts: *friend* moves a war from aggression to a dispute between two parties; *operative* moves a watchman from protected person to target; *natural* moves a grid failure from a design choice to an act of God; *consensus* moves one member's veto to twenty-seven members' decision; *market* moves a pensioner's eviction to an impersonal clearing price. In each case the word was doing the work that an argument should have done, and in each case the word was applied by the party that benefited from it.

**The designation is now being contested in all five.** Kyiv is asking a mediator to stop calling the aggressor a friend, and getting an answer about *"restraint"* and a *"grand master of politics."* MSF is asking who decided that two men in plastic chairs were a military target, and the answer is a press release issued by the air force that fired. Maryland's two million water customers are the finding that a storm is an infrastructure event, whatever the insurance industry calls it. Four member states have demonstrated that unanimity is not consensus but its opposite — a rule under which one government can speak for twenty-seven — and the chancellor has answered by insisting on the number, *"27 member states, not 23 or 24,"* which is a defence of the rule and an admission of its cost. And Spain is voting because a rent stopped being an abstraction on the day it put an 87-year-old woman in the street.

**And the cost still lands where the decision is not.** Two men at a doorway in Gaza City, one of them described by a friend as a peaceful man whose only concern was his wife, his two children and his mother. A Ukrainian family in a Kyiv block whose water did not come because a substation was hit and the pumps need power. Two million Marylanders on boiled water because a grid and a water utility keep separate books. A pensioner in Valencia who will vote for the party that promises to lower her rent and will get a coalition agreement about regional financing. And in Brussels, twenty-seven governments agreeing to meet again in December, which is what a decision looks like when everyone has decided to let the calendar decide.
