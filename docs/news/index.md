### 🌾 The Triple Chokepoint

???+ danger "Observed Cause"

If conditions persist, the agency expects **$727 million** in additional costs by **mid-2027**, putting assistance for **7.3 million** people at risk. *"This is taking money straight out of WFP's operations, directly translating into less food for fewer families,"* Lindberg said. The backdrop is **266 million** people facing acute hunger worldwide, with famine risk in parts of **Sudan**, **South Sudan** and **Somalia**; in Sudan the cost of a basic diet has risen **130 per cent since February**. He asked member states for three "red lines": no more famines, no more weaponisation of food, and no more attacks on humanitarians. And he named the cause exactly: *"This is not a problem of production. It is a political problem. Grain is available, but it is not reaching the people who need it."*

### ☣️ Ninety-Nine Per Cent

## The Pattern

Read the five together — **a missile that landed and was not intercepted**, **grain that exists and does not move**, **a territory that is contaminated and cannot be tested**, **a board that decided and was overruled**, **a court whose sentence was carried out before the ink dried** — and the common structure is not failure. It is that **every instrument in these five stories still exists and no longer functions.**

**The capacity is present in every case; what is missing is the act.** Poland's Patriots were powered up and the objects were allowed through, and the following night a drone was left alone for eight minutes by deliberate choice — a defence fully equipped that does not fire. The world's food supply is not short: the WFP director said it plainly, *"this is not a problem of production… Grain is available, but it is not reaching the people who need it"*. Gaza's contamination is measurable in principle — satellites can see it, UNEP can count 61 million tonnes of debris — but the laboratories that would confirm it are destroyed and the sampling equipment is restricted, so the number exists and the proof does not. Nvidia's board met, considered, and refused, and then reinstated its chief executive within eleven days on assurances from the government that was asking. And in Qom the appeals court, the defence lawyers and the sentence enforcement office all worked exactly as designed, which is how a flogging happened on the same day the lawyers asked for it to be postponed.

**The withholding is always a decision, and it is always someone's.** Not one of these five is a shortage, an accident or an act of nature. A signature holds sixty interceptors. A premium sets the price of a voyage. A destroyed laboratory is a war aim. A phone call reverses a board. An enforcement branch declines a postponement. Each is a person choosing, at a desk, in a building, on a date — and each choice is presented as a circumstance, because a circumstance has no author to be held to.

**And the cost is displaced onto whoever cannot withhold anything.** The family in Podkarpackie whose house the air defence declined to risk. The household in Yemen that imports ninety per cent of its food. The child in a tent site where storm water and sewage have merged. The labourer who will sort the asbestos by hand. The shareholder who bought the rally. The four musicians flogged beside a singer, and the 121 people waiting. None of them made a decision in any of these five stories, and all of them live inside the ones that were made.
