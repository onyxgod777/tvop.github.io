The law of cause and effect is the most precise and impartial of all creational laws. It does not punish or reward — **it reflects**. Every thought, feeling, word, and deed generates a specific vibrational cause. That cause accumulates energy, compresses into lived experience, and returns — amplified — as your destiny.

On this page, we track **current world events as observed causes** and project their **necessary effects** if those causes persist unchanged. This is not fortune-telling. It is **causal inference** — the same principle that lets a physicist predict a trajectory from position and velocity. See the cause, know the effect. Change the cause, and the effect dissolves.

???+ danger "Observed Cause"

A glacier collapse "probably triggered" the flood in the Rasuwa district north of Kathmandu, sending a surge down the **Bhote Koshi** river from Tibet into Nepal (Washington Post). The disaster is so vast that **rescuers were scouring Himalayan valleys for hundreds of missing foreigners — the largest contingent of them pilgrims travelling between Nepal and a sacred site in Tibet — while facing devastated roads and dozens of collapsed bridges that impede both the search and the delivery of relief** (NYT).

    The cause is a **climate-accelerated force now interacting with the fragility of the human terrain around it** — a warming Himalaya destabilising the glaciers and moraines that tower over steep river valleys shared by Nepal and the Chinese territory of Tibet, in a region still rebuilding from last year's glacier-burst flood. Compounding the natural driver is a slow, under-resourced preparedness: the search is being fought across destroyed infrastructure, and **experts warn of a possible second flood** as unstable ice and dammed water remain poised above the valley (BBC). What looks like a sudden natural catastrophe is the visible return of causes — warming emissions, glacial retreat, and the development of vulnerable valleys — set in motion across generations.

    - **The threat of a second flood compounds the first disaster** — the effect of unstable glacier ice and blocked watercourses remaining above the valley is the real possibility of another surge, forcing further evacuations, straining rescuers and aid groups already at the limit, and turning a single catastrophe into a sustained crisis
    - **Hundreds of foreign pilgrims and tourists magnify the international dimension** — the effect of a large contingent of pilgrims and travellers caught in the flood is a disaster that implicates many nations at once, complicating identification, repatriation and the diplomatic handling of the dead and missing
