### 🇺🇦 Two Alerts at 22:47

???+ danger "Observed Cause"

Radiation threat declared in Kyiv"*; then, **in Russian**, *"Hi! There will be a nuclear alert across Ukraine. Don't worry, it's Oreshnik flying"* — Oreshnik being Russia's intermediate-range ballistic missile, capable of nuclear or conventional warheads; then a chemical-attack warning; then a street-fighting warning. ### 🇸🇴 Handing Over After Twenty Years

???+ danger "Observed Cause"

The **UN's political mission** in Somalia is closing at the same time. Al-Shabab's insurgency has run for more than two decades and continues; Somali forces are to take "full responsibility" for security under a schedule written by the bodies now withdrawing. The same week, the UN's **MINURSO** peacekeeping mission in **Western Sahara** ended after **35 years** — a second long deployment closing in the same news cycle, with Morocco celebrating the defeat of the resolution that would have extended it.

## The Pattern

Look across these five. Though they span **a Doha draft that exists while both sides promise to resume shooting**; **an air-raid app that sent a fake nuclear warning eleven minutes before a ceasefire expired**; **a president handed 53 seats, 48 seats and 19 that neither bloc will name**; **a burning trouser leg that a minister called attempted murder and 18,000 people nobody counted**; and **109 soldiers walking out of Mogadishu on a schedule written in Kampala** — the common structure this week is not a document. It is **the clock**.

**In all five, the decisive object is a deadline set by someone who will not be the party that has to meet it.** Kherson's truce ended at **22:59**; the app's false warnings were timed to **22:47–22:49**; the Israeli mandate runs **42 days** from a certificate one man signs; AUSSOM's transition plan starts in **January** while the money is withdrawn **now**; the Patriots are approved for delivery in **"several years"** at 600 a year; and the Hormuz reopening is priced at **two days**. None of those clocks was set by the people whose safety depends on it. That is the shape of the week: **a timetable standing in for a decision**, adopted because a date can be published while a decision cannot be taken.

**And in each case the timetable has already begun to discharge obligations it was never agreed to carry.** The Kherson window did its work and closed; the evacuation happened, which proves localised truces are possible and makes their absence elsewhere a choice. The app's all-clear went out at 23:53 whether or not anyone had verified the hack — a warning system answering a false alarm with a false reassurance. AUSSOM's withdrawal began before its funding was replaced. The 42-day mandate will almost certainly produce a second candidate and then a third election, on schedule, which is a result the schedule itself guarantees. **A clock is a cause that arrives regardless of whether the parties act** — §117–118's accumulation, running on a calendar.

**Two of these are one mechanism at two scales: the entity that owns the warning owns the population.** Ukraine's state app and Russia's state app were both hacked within hours, and both populations received false nuclear warnings in their own language. In neither country is there a second, independent channel a citizen can verify. The same single-point-of-trust failure appears in the Doha file, where the only transit count either side will cite is its own — Iran's state television counting hulls, UKMTO counting 155 in and 122 out. **When the warning and the war come from the same hand, credibility is not a communications problem; it is the infrastructure.**

**And two are one mechanism at two scales: a mandate that exists on paper while the ground is held by someone else.** Herzog's 42 days and AUSSOM's 12 months are both grants of authority to parties that do not yet command the means to exercise it — 19 seats that will not be named in a coalition, 4,500 troops whose mission is being defunded. **In both, the transition is announced before the capability exists and the gap is bridged by the party that is leaving** — a caretaker governing Gaza, a bilateral Ugandan contingent outside AU command. A handover that keeps the hand is not a handover; it is a rebranding with a deadline attached.
