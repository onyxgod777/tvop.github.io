The earthquake — which struck at a **shallow depth of approximately 10 km** — caused widespread damage across Kyushu, knocking out power, disrupting transport, and sparking fires in the affected areas. **Survivors described violent shaking that lasted nearly a minute**, capturing the shock and horror of the event. The quake is the latest in a series of major seismic events along the **Pacific Ring of Fire** — the most tectonically active region on Earth.
