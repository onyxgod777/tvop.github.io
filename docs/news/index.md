### 🌍 An Age of Extremes Opens With a Merger

???+ danger "Observed Cause"
    **The conference opened on Monday with the atmosphere having already supplied the headline.** **COP31** opened in **Antalya** on **9 November** and runs to **20 November**. **UN Secretary-General António Guterres** said: *"A supersized El Niño is colliding with a planet already superheated by man made climate change… We have entered a new age of climate extremes."* He called on leaders to *"get serious by cutting emissions faster, accelerating a just transition from fossil fuels and delivering the finance and protection vulnerable countries need."*

    **The World Meteorological Organization's opening report put numbers where the rhetoric was.** Each of the **past 12 years** has been among the **12 hottest on record**, and **2026** is expected to be among the **four hottest**, with preliminary data putting it close to the top. The global average surface temperature from **January to September** was about **1.47°C above the pre-industrial average**. This year's **El Niño** is expected to be the strongest since at least **1950**, and the WMO forecasts tropical Pacific sea-surface temperatures **as much as 3.8°C above average** from now through January. *"What we are seeing gives new meaning to the phrase off the charts,"* said WMO chief **Celeste Saulo**, adding: *"We should prepare for another record-breaking year in 2027."* Global sea level is the highest on record; ice loss in 2025 ran at enough to fill **five Olympic pools every second**; and the number of countries with multi-hazard early warning systems rose from **55 in 2015 to 136 last year** — *"We cannot afford to fly blind,"* Saulo said.

    **The political frame carried in from the weekend is narrower than the science.** The UN's own conclusion is that holding warming to **1.5°C**, agreed at **COP21 in Paris in 2015**, is **no longer possible**, and that full implementation of current commitments implies up to **2.6°C**; the hosts' central aim is **green industrialisation**, with a target of raising electricity's share of global energy consumption to **35% by 2035** from roughly **20%** today. **Australia**, which leads the negotiations, has said the cover decision will not spring **"surprises"** — a commitment to the absence of surprise, which is the most honest description of a consensus process available.

    - **Finance and adaptation become the real currency, and the payer is chosen by the same unanimity rule that has blocked mitigation** — loss and damage, a just-transition mechanism, vulnerable countries with no delegation of their own; the direct effect is that the money is the only binding instrument left on the table; the compound consequence is that a transfer priced by unanimity is priced by the least willing contributor, so the instrument that survived is the one that can be emptied — and an empty fund is worse than no fund, because it has already been counted as a commitment

    *"How you look back on these old times today… is how your descendants will one day look back on the past, if they think about your time, your actions which are far from the creational laws, your unintellect and your irrationality, your contempt for humankind, the destruction of the nature, the eradication of many plants, fishes, birds, animals and other creatures and the destruction of the climate."* — *Goblet of the Truth*, Chapter 28, Verse 379

    **The hinge:** The cause is not emissions in the abstract; it is the choice to keep the measurable instruments small and the declared ambitions large. §28:379 is written from the far end of the chain — it describes a later generation looking back at exactly this decade and naming what it sees: contempt, destruction, and a climate destroyed knowingly. ## The Pattern

Look across these five — **a mandate made conditional on a speaker's chair**, **two days of talks with the ceasefire removed from the agenda**, **a tower hit in Riyadh answered by a village east of Sanaa**, **a gunman who died before a motive could be tested**, and **a climate conference opening under an El Niño that will be blamed for everything** — and the structure is not the limit this week. It is **the order**. Who decides what happens first.

**In every case the sequence is the policy, and it is being chosen by someone who will not bear the consequence.** Ohana controls the order of the Knesset's business, so the government waits on a chair; the Abu Dhabi format puts guarantees before a ceasefire, so an architecture is drafted for a line that does not yet exist; the exchange between Riyadh and Sanaa is ordered by whoever fires second, and the civilians are in the village because a tower was hit first; the Kraków file was ordered licence, then family, then square, and no one was assigned to the interval; and COP31 has ordered its agenda so that finance is discussed after a threshold has already been crossed, which turns a physical fact into a budget line.

**The tell is that in each case the party who set the order was not the party who would pay for it.** A speaker pays nothing for a week of delay. A president who says "a different shape" pays nothing for the phrase. A militia that fires from a village pays in the village. A licensing authority that never joined three registers pays nothing, because the man who would have been flagged is dead. And a presidency that promises no surprises pays nothing, because the surprises arrive as weather. Order is the cheapest power available and the least audited — which is exactly why it is never on the agenda of the meeting it decides.
