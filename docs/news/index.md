## The Pattern

Look across these five stories. Though they span **two tankers mined and a supertanker ablaze in the Strait of Hormuz as the UAE re-enters Yemen and Egypt seeks parliamentary war powers**; **more than a thousand Ukrainian drones setting St Petersburg's oil terminals alight while Washington pauses the Russia sanctions and floats more Russian gas for Europe**; **550,000 demonstrators and 1,800 arrests in France as a 143-year-old town hall burns**; **masked settlers beating European diplomats in Taybeh during the olive harvest while the prime minister's office calls the reporting a smear**; and **Hurricane Trudy closing on Mexico over saturated ground as category 4 Champi bears down on Guam** — the common structure is unmistakable.

**In every one of the five, the event described as news is the *effect*. The cause is upstream, human and named: a decision, by a patron and a militia to keep a strait closed and an airport under fire, and by Gulf states to answer the advance with cash and air cover rather than a settlement; a decision, by a government to pause a law it wrote and offer the aggressor's gas as an inducement to peace; a decision, by a state to fund schools as a remainder and then police the consequence with 150 injured officers; a decision, by settlers to attack a harvest and by a government to doubt the record rather than prosecute the act; and a decision, by societies and their governments about what is built, stocked and protected before the season arrives.**

In every case, the human cause is **a choice** — and therefore, the effect is **changeable**. Four of these five stories moved within a day, and each moved because somebody decided something: a waiver signed, a drone swarm launched, a mine laid, a convoy attacked. That is the same law running in both directions. Yesterday's page is the proof of the other direction: a 41.8% fall in Amazon deforestation within three years, produced not by weather but by enforcement — and still standing on a run-off nine days away.
