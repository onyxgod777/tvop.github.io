### 🇮🇱 Sixty-Seven to Forty-Six, and a Mandate That Waits for Sunday

???+ danger "Observed Cause"

### 🇵🇾 Forty-Four Votes, Three Million Depositors

???+ danger "Observed Cause"
    **A digital bank failed on a Tuesday and by Friday the president of a country was under house arrest, and the speed is the substance of the story.** **Ueno Bank**, which until last year counted President **Santiago Peña** among the shareholders of the company that owned it, was seized by **Paraguay's central bank** after its collapse. The failure left **more than half the population** without access to their accounts — an estimated **three million depositors** unable to reach savings and pensions — and produced queues down the streets of **Asunción** on Tuesday and Wednesday and protests across the country on Thursday, with police using tear gas and water cannons at earlier demonstrations.

    **The Senate chose removal over resignation, and the choice mattered.** Peña offered to step down on Thursday. Rather than accept a resignation, senators **impeached** him — **44 of 45 voting in favour**, one absent, including members of his own party — on a charge of **misconduct**, which stripped him of the immunity former presidents normally retain; he was arrested minutes after the vote and placed under house arrest. The impeachment charges allege **conflicts of interest, manipulation of regulatory oversight, diversion of pension funds, and an institutional cover-up involving the central bank**. Prosecutor **Silvia Margarita Gonzalez Vester** said he is due to be questioned on Saturday over **breach of trust, influence peddling and misappropriation of funds**. Senate president **Basilio Núñez** said Peña was found guilty of "dereliction of duty"; Senator **Rafael Filizzola** called it "the biggest corruption scandal in our democratic history — a tremendous blow to the state, to private citizens, and to the country's credibility and image". Peña, who says he never intended "to betray the trust you placed in me", denies wrongdoing; his lawyer **Ricardo Preda** called the accusations "reckless", "irresponsible and opportunistic". Vice-president **Pedro Alliana** was sworn in as interim president until **2028** and promised that "compatriots who are now going through a difficult time as a result of the financial collapse will be fully compensated".

??? warning "Projected Effect (if cause persists)"

    - **"Fully compensated" is a promise with no funding line, made by a president nobody elected** — three million depositors, half the population, an interim term to 2028; the direct effect is that the state has assumed a liability larger than any recent budget item; the compound consequence is that the choice is between debt, inflation and instalments, and each produces a different political future — so the most consequential economic decision in Paraguay this decade will be taken by a caretaker, which is the kind of decision a caretaker is least able to make and least able to refuse
    - **A digital bank has no branches to queue at, so the loss was invisible until it was total** — balances reconciled in an app, a central bank seizure, then nothing; the direct effect is that the failure arrived without the run that usually warns of one; the compound consequence is that the same architecture is being sold across the region as financial inclusion, and the failure will be read as a verdict on digital banking rather than on supervision — the wrong lesson, and the one that will be learned, because it requires no one to examine the regulator

### 🇪🇺 Hoaxes Above, Thermostats Below

???+ danger "Observed Cause"

No explosives were found on any aircraft.     **The signature is the weakest part of the evidence and the only part anyone has.** Emailed threats to airports in **Rome** and **Sofia** were signed "fighters of the **Male State**", according to sources at **Aeroporti di Roma** and a text seen by **AFP**. Male State is a far-right anti-feminist movement founded in **2016** by **Vladislav Pozdnyakov** and banned in Russia. Pozdnyakov, on Telegram, mocked the suggestion of involvement — "What Male State? Where have you seen it? It hasn't existed for many years" — and asked the **BBC Russian Service** whether, if a threat were signed in Putin's name tomorrow, they would ask the president; the **Guardian** notes he did not claim responsibility. The **FT** reports European security agencies are probing whether the hoaxes started in Russia; **at least 25 flights** were disrupted on Thursday and seven planes searched or diverted on Tuesday.

    **The refrigeration attacks are the half of the campaign with a physical target.** France's **Blood Establishment (EFS)** confirmed that **temperature and defrosting thresholds were raised to extremely high levels** at **three** of its plasma cold-storage sites, in an incident that hit a refrigeration-equipment supplier, **Coolworld**; authorities said no blood products or samples were compromised. It follows mid-October attacks in which fridges and freezers at **hundreds** of supermarkets and independent stores in **France, Belgium and the Netherlands** were remotely driven to **50°C**, and a second round this week across at least eight countries; a butcher in **Kifisia**, Athens, told **Skai TV** his unit was set to **150°C** and took a technician two hours to recover, after which he switched off its Wi-Fi. French investigators pointed to obsolete remote-control modules from Denmark's **Danfoss** and the US firm **Copeland**; Danfoss said most affected systems were older products no longer receiving security updates, Copeland cited a vulnerability in discontinued controllers. The UK's **NCSC** head **Richard Horne** said the French fridges were "connected directly to the internet with default passwords"; **Darktrace's Nicole Carignan** put it as "technical debt coming due" — "nobody broke into these refrigeration systems with a zero-day". This is the week the **European Commission** published preparedness guidance urging households to hold **72 hours** of supplies, and the week Britain's **Be Ready** advice says the same.

??? warning "Projected Effect (if cause persists)"

    - **The blood-bank incident was caught by thresholds and monitoring, and that is the argument for both** — raised set-points detected, samples intact, a supplier named; the direct effect is that the defence worked at the only layer that could work; the compound consequence is that the success is invisible, because nobody was harmed, so nobody will fund the next inspection — while the spoiled food and the cancelled flights are visible and will be funded; the money will therefore flow to the response and not to the inventory, which is the wrong side of the failure
    - **An attribution vacuum is an invitation, not a puzzle** — a banned movement's name signed to threats its founder denies, agencies tracing origin, a government saying it does not know whether this is individuals or a campaign; the direct effect is that any actor can now act under a name it did not invent; the compound consequence is that retaliation taken on the strength of a signature is retaliation taken on a forgery, which is precisely the escalation a hybrid campaign exists to produce — and the defence against it is a published attribution standard, so that a name in an email is filed as a claim and not as a culprit

## The Pattern

Look across these five — **a 67-vote majority that cannot assemble a government**, **36 ballistic missiles fired four days before the talks they are meant to make possible**, **a trillion-dollar repricing of an industry whose regulating bill has not been drafted**, **a president impeached in a day over a bank that has not been audited**, and **a week of threats attributed to a movement its founder says does not exist** — and the structure is not displacement, as it was last week. It is **the proxy**: in all five, something easy to produce and hard to falsify is standing in for the thing it is supposed to represent.

**A recommendation is standing in for a coalition. A barrage is standing in for a negotiating position. A share price is standing in for a policy. An impeachment is standing in for an audit. A signature is standing in for an attribution.** Each proxy is cheaper than its original, each is available immediately, and each can be counted, quoted or photographed — which is exactly why it displaces the slower thing. The Knesset has 67 names and no government. The market has a verdict and no statute. The Senate has a verdict and no audit. Europe has a culprit's name and no culprit.

**The tell is that in every case the proxy was produced faster than the original could have been, and the speed was reported as the achievement.** A mandate to be granted on Sunday rather than after the coalition was built; a strike package flown before the table was set; a sell-off completed before the bill was written; an impeachment voted before the regulator was questioned; a threat signed before anyone checked who signs. **In each case the instrument that would convert the proxy into the thing itself — a coalition agreement, a ceasefire window, a published cost per unit, an audit, an attribution standard — is the one instrument nobody has produced, because producing it takes longer than the deadline allows.**

**And two of these are the same mechanism at opposite ends of one wire.** A bomb threat emailed to an airport and a thermostat command sent to a blood bank are both cases of a system accepting an instruction because it was never designed to ask who was giving it — one at the layer of a human procedure, one at the layer of a default password. **The first is answered by a security assessment every time; the second was answered once, by a threshold alarm, and nobody noticed that the alarm is the only thing standing between a plasma store and 50°C.** The finding of the week is not that Europe is under attack. It is that the defences which held had a number attached — a temperature set-point, a 61-signature quorum, a 4.1% unemployment range — and the ones that failed relied on someone's judgment about a name.

What has dropped away is instructive. **Iran is held**: no new imagery of the Karaj and Shahroud excavations this cycle, the "seven demands" unchanged, and the only date that now matters is the same 10 November that belongs to Abu Dhabi — if the nuclear file moves this month it will move in the week of the Ukrainian one, which is a coincidence worth watching rather than a pattern worth asserting. **The EU–Rwanda return hubs are held**: the five states are reported to be closing in on a plan, but the regulation still awaits formal adoption by the Council, and this page's question — a named monitor with a remedy — remains unanswered. **California's 548 commutations are closed**: final, unreviewable, and no longer a live cause. **Hong Kong and Macau belong in the case register**, not in a fourth week of the same analysis.
