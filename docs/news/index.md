Every thing that exists becomes and passes — change is the only constant, and it begins with the causes you choose."*
>
> — *Goblet of the Truth*, §110–111, §240

The law of cause and effect is the most precise and impartial of all creational laws. It does not punish or reward — **it reflects**. Every thought, feeling, word, and deed generates a specific vibrational cause. That cause accumulates energy, compresses into lived experience, and returns — amplified — as your destiny.

On this page, we track **current world events as observed causes** and project their **necessary effects** if those causes persist unchanged. This is not fortune-telling. It is **causal inference** — the same principle that lets a physicist predict a trajectory from position and velocity. See the cause, know the effect. Change the cause, and the effect dissolves.

### 🇺🇦 Russia Warns Britain of "Consequences" After British-Made Drones Strike Deep Inside Russia for the First Time — a New Escalation With a NATO Ally

???+ danger "Observed Cause"
    On August 17–18, 2026, **the Russian embassy to the UK accused Britain of "deliberately opting for an escalation of the Ukraine crisis"** and warned that "the deeper its involvement in the conflict… the higher the price it will pay" — after confirmation that **British-made drones were used in recent strikes on military and industrial targets in mainland Russia for the first time**, according to the BBC, Reuters, The Guardian and The Times. The UK Ministry of Defence replied that it stood "shoulder to shoulder" with Ukraine and that "Russia should be in no doubt about the resolve of this government to stand against Russian aggression." In the same window, **Ukraine launched some 620 drones toward the Moscow region overnight** (Russian officials said air defences destroyed more than 180), wounding several people — including a 10-year-old girl — and damaging a logistics complex of the online retailer Wildberries, which Ukraine has been targeting to hit Russia's economy and morale; Russian attacks in turn killed at least 17 people in Ukraine, including 10 villagers in Pechenihy in the north-east, as Kyiv came under five waves of attack overnight. The cause is not any single launch but the **decision by both leaderships — and now their backers — that answering the other's growing reach with a deeper blow serves their aims**, a spiral in which a NATO member's weapons striking the Russian heartland has become the new threshold.

??? warning "Projected Effect (if cause persists)"

    - **Economic and morale targets turn the whole country into the battlefield** — Ukraine's strikes on oil refineries, fuel logistics and the Wildberries network show the war shifting toward economic attrition inside Russia, so the effect is that the conflict's cost is paid ever further from the front line, in disrupted domestic fuel supply, commerce and civilian life, and the same logic answers back in Russian strikes on Ukrainian towns
    - **Each round entrenches the political stake in going deeper** — a UK government that has vowed to stand "shoulder to shoulder" and a Kremlin that has promised London a price cannot easily step back; the effect is that escalation becomes self-reinforcing, because the capital invested in striking deeper is repaid only by striking deeper, not by stopping
    - **The harder the West is seen to fight by proxy, the further the diplomatic track recedes** — the more Moscow frames this as a NATO war, the less it will engage in any negotiated off-ramp; the effect is that the endgame is written on the battlefield and at the brink, not at a table, and Europe's bid for a role in eventual talks grows more urgent precisely as the violence widens

### 🇮🇷 60-Day US–Iran Deal Expires Without a Final Agreement as Trump Threatens to Bomb Oman — a US Ally — Over the Strait of Hormuz

???+ danger "Observed Cause"
    On August 17–18, 2026, **the 60-day US–Iran memorandum of understanding expired without a broad deal**, and **Donald Trump threatened to bomb Oman — a US ally — if it "gets in the way"** of his administration's talks with Iran, telling Fox News in a phone interview, "If Oman gets in the way, we'll bomb the shit out of them" (reported by Fox's Trey Yingst). Asked by the BBC whether he would seek an extension of the MoU, Trump replied: "No." The Strait of Hormuz — through which roughly a fifth of the world's oil and liquefied natural gas usually passes — has been largely closed for nearly six months since the US and Israel attacked Iran in February. **Oman, which borders the southern side of the strait and says it is neutral**, has been negotiating separately with Tehran to reopen the waterway; Iranian officials said they were close to finalising a deal with Oman, with the foreign ministry spokesman saying "an understanding has been reached regarding the map of the transit route," while Reuters reported Tehran is seeking fees of 5–7% of the value of cargoes. Trump also claimed a direct backchannel with Iran's Revolutionary Guards — a claim the IRGC denied — and said he was "in no hurry" to end the war ("They're good poker players, but they're dying"). The cause is the **choice by both capitals to keep treating "victory" and maximal pressure as more attainable than a durable deal** — and now, Washington extending that same coercion even to the ally and neutral most able to reopen the waterway.

??? warning "Projected Effect (if cause persists)"
    - **Threatening the mediator destroys the path to the very outcome claimed as the goal** — Oman is the interlocutor best placed to get ships moving through Hormuz, so menacing it removes the channel that could end the economic stranglehold; the effect is that the war's stated purpose (reopening the strait, limiting Iran's nuclear program) is pushed further away by the means chosen to reach it
    - **The Hormuz economic weapon stays cocked against the whole world** — with oil and LNG flows, insurance, shipping lanes and global energy prices held hostage to the confrontation, the effect is that the war's cost is paid in commerce by nations that chose none of it, and every stalled day of negotiation prolongs that global levy
    - **A quagmire with depleted levers raises the danger of miscalculation** — with interceptor stocks drawn down, strategic reserves drained and carriers like the USS Abraham Lincoln (deployed since December, reportedly contending with food shortages, broken plumbing and exhaustion) stretched past planned limits, the effect is that each side's confidence in its own deterrent is lowest precisely when the rhetoric is highest, a combination that historically invites error more than restraint
    - **Treating allies and neutrals as disposable corrodes the wider alliance web** — Oman, and every Gulf state and ally watching, draws the lesson that a partner who prices loyalty and threatens friends is a risky one; the effect is a structural drift toward diversified partnerships that outlasts any single deal
    - **Every regional front stays braided to this hub** — Gaza, Lebanon, Yemen and even the US–South Korea alliance are linked to the Iran confrontation (Seoul's refusal to join it is why Washington cut its drills), so the effect is that the collapse of this track simultaneously destabilises the region's other ceasefires and the credibility of alliances far from the Gulf

    *"Forgive your culprits (those who are culpable to you) their culpabilities, because they load this upon themselves since they are not knowing of the truth and therefore do not know what they are doing; therefore do not allow the lust for revenge to rise up in you, but rather consider that revenge is unright and any unright is evil."* — Chapter 19, Verse 102

    **The hinge:** The cause is not the expired deadline — it is the **decision by Washington and Tehran that maximal pressure, threats and "victory" serve their ends better than a workable deal**, and the decision to aim that coercion at the very allies and neutrals who could mediate. The hinge is whether **both capitals** change that cause — whether they treat the other's exhaustion, and the world's paid price in oil and shipping, as reasons to build a durable settlement rather than weaknesses to exploit, and whether Washington recognises that threatening the mediator is the surest way to keep the strait closed. It is whether the **wider world**, which pays the economic cost, insists that the Gulf's shipping lanes are not the private battlefield of two governments. A warning unheeded only becomes fate when no one changes the cause; the causes that would revive the deal remain free choices.

### 🇮🇩 Indonesia Quake Death Toll Rises Past 68 as Thousands Await Aid on the Nation's Independence Day — a Test of Preparedness on the Ring of Fire

???+ danger "Observed Cause"
    On August 17–18, 2026, **the death toll from the 7.7-magnitude earthquake that struck Indonesia's Flores island on Saturday climbed past 68**, up from about 53 a day earlier, as rescuers reached cut-off valleys, according to CBS News, CBC, Reuters, AP and France 24. **Thousands of evacuees remained waiting for help, sleeping outdoors through sweltering days and frigid nights**, even as the country marked its Independence Day on August 17 — one of the strongest tremors the area has experienced in decades, followed by a magnitude-5.9 aftershock that shook already-damaged structures. Landslides have severed access roads, the roof of Ruteng Regional General Hospital caved in leaving its operating room, delivery room and pharmacy defunct, and supplies are dwindling; more than 1,000 houses and dozens of health facilities, schools and government offices have been damaged. Indonesia sits on the Pacific Ring of Fire, so earthquakes are a structural reality; but the **severity of the human cost is set by the built environment, preparedness and response capacity** that communities invested before the ground moved — and by the speed with which aid now reaches a road-cut island.

??? warning "Projected Effect (if cause persists)"
    - **The toll is set by response speed, not by the tremor — and it is still climbing** — with remote valleys cut off by landslides and the death count rising as rescuers reach them, the effect is that the final number will be written in real time by aid and rescue capacity, and a high toll signals a preparedness gap that will exact a price again in the next tremor
    - **Aftershocks extend the danger well beyond the main event** — a region still digging through rubble now faces renewed shaking, further landslides and unstable hospitals; the effect is that this is a sustained period of risk, not a single moment, and relief must be continuous rather than episodic
    - **Severed infrastructure isolates the hardest-hit areas exactly when hours decide survival** — with roads buried and supplies dwindling, the effect is that remote communities may go days without medical care or food, and the true toll in the mountains may emerge slowly, long after the headlines move on
    - **A health system forced into tents compounds every injury** — with the main hospital's operating room, delivery room and pharmacy defunct, the effect is that even survivable injuries become life-threatening, and maternal, surgical and chronic care collapse precisely when a disaster demands them most
    - **The Ring of Fire makes this a recurring cause, not a one-off** — Indonesia will experience more severe earthquakes; the effect is that every relief effort is also a test of whether the lessons of seismic building codes, early warning and institutionalised preparedness are learned, or whether each disaster is treated as an isolated tragedy rather than the predictable return of an ongoing cause

    *"Therefore you shall esteem and love people of your kind (next ones/fellow human beings) and always give them a good example, as well as helping them in tribulation and misery, because truly whatever you do to people of your kind (next ones/fellow human beings), you also do for yourselves."* — Chapter 19, Verse 92

    **The hinge:** The cause is not the earthquake — the tremor is the effect of tectonic forces no human set in motion; **the death toll and the aid crisis are the effects of human causes: how the built environment was designed, how prepared the institutions were, and how fast the response now moves**. The hinge is whether **Indonesia and the international community** treat this event as the warning it is — delivering aid to the road-cut island at the speed the survivors need, and investing in seismic building codes, retrofitting hospitals and schools, early-warning systems and public preparedness so the next quake of equal force claims far fewer lives. The ground will move again; whether it again takes dozens of lives and leaves thousands sleeping outside depends on the causes we set in the years between.

### 🇨🇦 Canada and the US Race the Clock to Avert Trump's 50% Tariffs as Auto-Sector Talks Stall and Carney Seeks a Last-Minute Call

???+ danger "Observed Cause"
    On August 18, 2026, **Canada was racing the clock to avoid new US tariffs set to take effect at midnight Tuesday (August 19)**, with **Prime Minister Mark Carney calling the last-minute negotiations "delicate" and "intense"** and saying he expects to speak with President Trump before the deadline, according to the NYT, Financial Times, BBC, CBC, Bloomberg and the Washington Post. Finance Minister Dominic LeBlanc said the "job is not yet done" after trade meetings, while Bloomberg reported the talks are **bogged down over auto-sector tariffs** — a deeply integrated cross-border industry. The Trump administration has threatened **50% tariffs** on Canadian goods, and the two sides were reportedly aiming for a deal to avert them even as the clock ran out. The cause is not any single tariff but the **decision to wield economic coercion as an instrument of policy** — treating a close ally's trade as a weapon to be brandished, renegotiated and threatened anew, so that the relationship itself is re-priced around the standing threat of punishment.

??? warning "Projected Effect (if cause persists)"
    - **Coercion as a standing policy converts trust into a hostage negotiation** — even if a deal is struck at the deadline, the effect is that Canada and every other trading partner must henceforth plan around the possibility that the threat returns, so the relationship's stability is replaced by permanent uncertainty
    - **The auto sector carries the deepest integration and the sharpest pain** — with components crossing the border many times before assembly, the effect of tariffs is to disrupt continental supply chains, raise production costs and push prices up on both sides, turning a political threat into consumer and industrial costs almost immediately
    - **Allies respond to a partner who prices loyalty by diversifying away from it** — just as other allies have drawn the lesson from similar coercion, the effect is a structural drift by Canada (and others) toward alternative markets, energy and supply routes that outlasts any single tariff fight and permanently reduces dependence
    - **The threat itself becomes the economy's weather** — businesses defer investment, hedge, stockpile and re-price around the deadline; the effect is that the mere anticipation of tariffs, not just their imposition, becomes an economic drag on jobs, growth and confidence in both countries
    - **Retaliation and escalation can compound into a broader trade spiral** — if the deadline passes without a deal, the effect is that tariffs beget counter-tariffs, raising costs and deepening the very resentment that each side's politics feeds on, and extending the damage beyond North America into global supply chains

    *"Anyone who undertakes haggling (usury/greed) in any form of commerce and unrightfully demands a markup (interest or price increase) shall be exposed to the punishment of recompense through the authority or jurisdiction, so that the disadvantaged ones do not suffer any loss of goods and chattels, possessions and fortune."* — Chapter 2, Verse 388

    **The hinge:** The cause is not the tariff deadline — it is the **decision to treat economic exchange as a weapon and an ally's trade as a threat to be priced and punished**. The hinge is whether **Washington** changes that cause — whether it recognises that a relationship built on coercion is repaid with the very distrust it feeds on, and whether the two governments convert the deadline into a durable, good-faith framework rather than a pause before the next threat. It is whether **Canada and the US** treat the auto sector and the wider trade relationship as a shared value to be protected rather than a ledger to be squeezed. Economic strength is a choice of causes, not a fixed fate: use the threat as a lever, and the account comes due in eroded trust and higher costs for all; treat the exchange as mutual, and it holds.

### 🇵🇭 A Teen Livestreams a School Shooting in Zamboanga City — Killing a Classmate and Himself — as Southeast Asia Wrestles With Youth, Guns and Online Violence

???+ danger "Observed Cause"
    On August 18, 2026, **a student at a Catholic junior high school in Zamboanga City, in the southern Philippines, shot and killed a classmate before fatally turning the gun on himself**, in what officials said was a rare school shooting in the country, according to the BBC, AP, Reuters, CNN and Al Jazeera. Mayor Khymer Adan Olaso said the alleged shooter **sneaked a .45-calibre pistol into a classroom**, fired at the teacher seated at the table (missing), moved to another classroom and shot a student who later died, then shot himself; **alleged livestream footage of the attack — a first-person view of a gun barrel moving down a hallway — was circulated by local media**, per AFP. Authorities said there were no more active shooters. The incident comes **two months after a high school shooting in Tacloban City left three students dead and 20 wounded** — a case in which one of the two teenage suspects was found to have regularly played an explicitly violent online game. Across Southeast Asia, authorities are looking at restricting social media use for minors, citing possible ill effects. The causes of such an act are multiple and human: **access to firearms, a young person's unaddressed distress, exposure to violent content, and now a platform that broadcasts the act of violence in real time to a global audience** — an amplification loop that turns one teenager's despair into a spectacle millions can watch.

??? warning "Projected Effect (if cause persists)"
    - **Livestreaming converts a local tragedy into a global blueprint** — broadcasting the act in real time hands the perpetrator an audience that the next troubled individual can see and imitate; the effect is that the spectacle itself becomes part of the cause, lowering the threshold for a copycat who seeks the same reach
    - **The combination of accessible firearms and available audiences raises regional copycat risk** — in a region where social media is ubiquitous and gun access remains possible, the effect is that one act can seed others, and the two months between Tacloban and Zamboanga show the pattern is already repeating
    - **Policy is crystallising around restricting the platform, not just the person** — Southeast Asian governments moving to restrict minors' social media reflect a shift from intervening in individual distress to regulating the medium; the effect is that the debate over youth and screens becomes a live policy front, with consequences for privacy, digital access and how young people are governed online
    - **The deepest causal terrain is youth wellbeing, not the weapon alone** — when a shooter is found to have steeped in violent games and an act is livestreamed for an audience, the effect is that the real remedy lies in what children are taught, shown and given to process — the content and connection (or lack of it) that shapes a young mind before it reaches for a gun
    - **Every repetition hardens public fear and the appetite for control** — two school shootings in two months in a country where such events were rare will accelerate calls for stricter gun laws and tighter platform regulation; the effect is that the reaction to the symptom, however justified, may miss the cause unless it also addresses the isolation and exposure feeding it

    *"You shall teach your children so that they are firmly grounded (taught) in the knowledge of all things of the existence (life) and in the teaching of the truth, the teaching of the spirit, the teaching of the life, therefore also in the things of the life, dying and death, so that they do not worry about life and do not have one iota of uncertainty and so that the greatest reward of the life may be granted to them."* — Chapter 4, Verse 209

    **The hinge:** The cause is not the pistol — a weapon is only the last instrument; **the cause is what a young person is taught, shown and left alone with: unaddressed distress, exposure to violent content, easy access to a gun, and a platform that rewards the act with an audience**. The hinge is whether **families, schools, platforms and governments** change that cause — whether a troubled teenager is reached before the moment of decision, whether violent content and unmoderated broadcasts are curbed, whether firearms stay out of classrooms, and whether the digital world children inherit is built to connect rather than to amplify despair and violence.
