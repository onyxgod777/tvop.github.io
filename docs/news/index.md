### 🇮🇱 The Ward Was Hit at Midnight, and the Toll Is Now the Detail

### 🇺🇦 The Blessing Arrived Before the Paper

Ireland's **Taoiseach Micheál Martin**, hosting the leaders in Dublin, said the region's security had been *"fundamentally reshaped"* by the war: *"Many member states feel an existential threat… and the potential, if Ukraine was to fall, for further aggression."*

### 🤖 The Founder Left at Lunchtime; the First Chair Arrived With Him

???+ danger "Observed Cause"

### 🌍 1.5 Is Declared Dead in the Hall That Keeps It Alive

???+ danger "Observed Cause"
    **The summit opened in the knowledge that its central target will be missed, and its response was to keep saying the target's name.** **COP31** is meeting in **Antalya** from **9 to 20 November**, co-hosted by **Türkiye** and **Australia** — the first climate conference since the **United Nations** told the world that warming will exceed **1.5°C**, and that even with dramatic emission cuts starting now the best available outcome is about **1.8°C**, with a theoretical return to 1.5 only after overshoot and only with carbon removal at a scale nobody has demonstrated. On Thursday the independent **Climate Action Tracker** added the number that made the room quiet: progress in slowing warming has **essentially stalled for five years** — the first five years after Paris trimmed a full degree Celsius from projections, and nothing has moved since — leaving the world *"headed toward an irrecoverable overshoot of 1.5 degrees Celsius."* **Ursula von der Leyen** told leaders *"we are on course to overshoot… so we must double down."* **Hilda Heine**, president of the **Marshall Islands**, said *"let's show that the fight for 1.5C is not over"* and then described what overshoot means: *"waves eating away our shores, flooding that destroys towns, schools, hospitals."*

    **The gap between the speeches and the portfolios is the story, and it is measurable.** **António Guterres** told leaders that developed countries *"cannot keep expanding fossil fuel production while claiming to be committed to 1.5 degrees"* — and, per Politico's reporting from the hall, leaders did exactly that immediately afterwards. **Australia**, a co-host, is expanding investment in coal and natural gas while its climate minister **Chris Bowen** urges *"urgency, solidarity and resolve."* **Recep Tayyip Erdoğan** told the summit that *"with every minute that we fail to achieve the targets set out in the Paris Agreement, the risks and costs we face continue to grow"* — two days after celebrating the discovery of **two new oil fields**. The UK's climate minister **Katie White** said *"breaching 1.5 isn't where we down tools and give up,"* and a day later her department approved a major natural gas project. The **United States**, the largest historical emitter, has withdrawn from the Paris Agreement and is **not in the room**. The compromise emerging is not a target but a pollutant: **Mia Mottley**, prime minister of Barbados, argued that cutting **methane** by nearly half by 2030 could avoid almost **0.3°C** of warming by the 2040s and save **250,000 lives a year** — *"this buys us the time to get back to the 1.5 degrees"* — and European negotiators say a methane response is a likely element of the cover decision due by **20 November**.

    - **Methane is the one lever that pays within a decade, and it is being handled as an optional extra** — nearly half by 2030 avoids roughly 0.3°C and 250,000 deaths a year, in a week when both the US and Europe are weakening methane regulations; the direct effect is that the cheapest available cooling is left on the table; the compound consequence is that the overshoot the scientists now consider locked in will be deeper and longer than it needed to be — and the difference is not technological, it is a measurement-and-enforcement problem on leaks that satellites can already see
    - **Island states are negotiating for survival with a currency the room no longer accepts** — the Marshall Islands describing flooded hospitals, Barbados proposing a movement of *"1.5 billion voices,"* the **ICJ** having declared 1.5 the primary goal; the direct effect is that their legal and moral position strengthens as their physical position weakens; the compound consequence is that a loss-and-damage architecture built on a target that has been exceeded becomes a compensation regime — and compensation, unlike mitigation, is paid after the land is gone
    - **A summit measured by whether a text exists will produce a text, and the text will be the achievement** — a cover decision due 20 November, a chaired process, 190 delegations and a host with new oil fields; the direct effect is that the final document's language becomes the success metric; the compound consequence is that each COP's institutional survival depends on declaring the previous one insufficient — a permanent cycle of promise and deferral that consumes the decade in which the overshoot could still have been shortened, and trains the public to read the communiqué instead of the concentration

### 🦠 Eleven Thousand Cases Behind a Road That Will Not Open

???+ danger "Observed Cause"
    **The largest Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of the Congo's history is outpacing the response in the one province where the response cannot reach.** As of **11 November**, the DRC had reported **11,114 confirmed cases** and **5,433 deaths** from Ebola disease caused by **Bundibugyo virus**, according to the **ECDC** — a case fatality rate of roughly **49 per cent** — across **eight provinces**: Ituri, Nord-Kivu, Sud-Kivu, Tshopo, Haut-Uélé, Bas-Uélé, Sud-Ubangi and Nord-Ubangi. The **WHO**'s latest disease outbreak notice counted **1,463 new confirmed cases in the 21 days to 3 November** — about **70 a day** — with **Ituri** accounting for **710** of them and **Nord-Kivu 640**; Ituri alone has recorded **7,485 cases and 3,532 deaths**, with cases in **28 of its 36 health zones**. The outbreak began in **May 2026** and was declared a **public health emergency of international concern**. It has crossed borders: **Kenya** reported **nine** cases including **two deaths**, all linked to a single importation, and has now completed 21 days of contact quarantine with seven recoveries; **Nigeria** reported one case, medically evacuated to **Switzerland**, with **62** contacts traced; **Uganda**'s outbreak has ended. Four cases linked to the DRC outbreak have been evacuated or imported into the **EU/EEA** — two to Germany, one to the Netherlands, one to France — plus a precautionary evacuation to the UK, every one of them a health or humanitarian worker.

    **The new epicentre is defined by violence rather than by epidemiology.** The **New York Times** reports from the outbreak's new centre that it is *"a region scarred by rebel violence"* and *"largely isolated from aid by an armed group that has killed and kidnapped aid workers"* — its headline sets the two threats side by side: *"Caught Between Killers: Ebola on One Side, Islamic State on the Other."* Health workers at the epicentre are reported to be working with little pay and little rest; treatment centres have been attacked, and residents burned one in May as anger over the outbreak grew. The virus is **Bundibugyo**, not Zaire ebolavirus — the species against which the stockpiled vaccines and antibody treatments were developed — and the case fatality now recorded is far above the roughly 17 per cent reported in the outbreak's early weeks. **Kenya** is counting down to the end of its own outbreak; **Congo** is counting cases it cannot reach.

??? warning "Projected Effect (if cause persists)"

    - **A 49 per cent case fatality rate is a statement about access, not about virulence** — 5,433 deaths among 11,114 confirmed cases, against about 17 per cent early in the outbreak; the direct effect is that people arrive at treatment late or not at all; the compound consequence is that each untreated death is also an unsafe burial, which is the highest-transmission event in an Ebola outbreak — so the mortality rate and the infection rate reinforce each other, and both are governed by the same closed road
    - **Bundibugyo is the species the stockpile was not built for** — vaccines and therapeutics licensed and pre-positioned against Zaire ebolavirus do not straightforwardly cover a different species, and the trials that would answer the question need the access the response does not have; the direct effect is that the classic ring-vaccination playbook cannot simply be executed; the compound consequence is that the outbreak becomes a test case for how the world handles a haemorrhagic fever whose countermeasures are experimental — and the answer will be produced in Ituri, under fire, or not at all
    - **Health workers are the system, and they are being killed, kidnapped and left unpaid** — aid workers killed and kidnapped by the armed group, staff at the epicentre working with little pay or rest, a treatment centre burned by residents in May; the direct effect is that each attack removes capacity that cannot be replaced quickly, because Ebola responders are trained and scarce; the compound consequence is that the outbreak selects for the collapse of trust — communities that have seen a centre burned and a worker kidnapped do not report symptoms, and an outbreak that is not reported is an outbreak that is not traced
    - **Four evacuations to Europe are the outbreak's early-warning system, and they are all healthcare workers** — Germany twice, the Netherlands, France, plus a UK precautionary evacuation, in an outbreak that has already reached Kenya and Nigeria; the direct effect is that European hospital preparedness is now live rather than theoretical; the compound consequence is that the political response to the next imported case will be shaped by border policy rather than by epidemiology — and a travel ban on the region, the most likely political reaction, would close the last air bridge the response depends on

The provision Ituri needs is not primarily money. ## The Pattern

Look across these five — **a missile through a hospital's surgery department whose use only the belligerents can describe**, **a blessing for European participation granted in Moscow and written nowhere**, **a founder removed and a chair appointed in a single press release**, **a temperature target declared unreachable in the hall built to reach it**, and **eleven thousand Ebola cases behind a road an armed group controls** — and the structure of this week is not a decision. It is an **authorisation without an operating procedure**.

**Each of the five produced the word and withheld the mechanism.** In Khan Younis the word is *"looking into reports"* — an inquiry with no named investigator, no scope and no date. In Moscow the word is *"willing to give it a try"* — a permission with no document, which is why Europe is now negotiating with itself about who sits in the chair. In Santa Clara the word is a resignation and a first-ever chair, delivered the same day, with the investigation's scope and the recusals still unpublished. In Antalya the word is *1.5*, spoken by every leader in the room and contradicted by their own approvals within twenty-four hours. In Ituri the word is a case count, published weekly, describing a region the responders cannot enter.

**The tell is where each of them locates the missing piece.** Four of the five are waiting on a signature that costs the signatory nothing: a forensic protocol with ICRC access, a named European representative with a written mandate, a published scope and recusal list, a quantified methane clause with a reporting body. The fifth — Ituri — is waiting on a negotiation that costs something, because the party holding the road is armed and has already killed the people coming down it. That asymmetry is the week's real information: the documents that require only willingness are the ones not being written, and the one that requires courage is the one nobody is attempting.

**What this does to causality is precise.** An authorisation without a procedure does not fail loudly; it converts into a cost that falls on whoever cannot read the document. The patient in the surgical ward. The prisoner whose exchange has no date. The pension holder whose index fund carries the concentration. The islander whose target has been declared unreachable. The contact tracer in a health zone with no convoy. §110's instruction is operational here in a way that is easy to miss: the cause to be changed is not the missile, the war, the affair, the emissions or the virus. It is the missing second page — the clause that names who certifies, who verifies, who reports, and by when. Each of those clauses is drafting work. None requires a party to lose. All require a party to accept a witness, which is the one concession that costs nothing and is therefore the one nobody offers.
