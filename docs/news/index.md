### 🔥 The Maintenance Contract

???+ danger "Observed Cause"

### 🇺🇳 The Ceiling Is the Offer

???+ danger "Observed Cause"
    **The tariff announcement that read as a shock on Thursday is being read on Friday as a legal repair job, and the tell is that the numbers are ceilings.** The **US Trade Representative's** proposed duties on fourteen jurisdictions — **China** up to **40 per cent**, **Vietnam** up to **35**, and the EU, Japan, India, Korea, Taiwan, Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia, Cambodia, Singapore, Switzerland and Bangladesh at **20 or 25** — do not take effect until **January or possibly February**, after a consultation period. The BBC's economics editor, **Faisal Islam**, notes that *"there is something rather tentative about this latest exercise with talk of further negotiations,"* and quotes USTR **Jamieson Greer** telling CNBC: *"We expect to have a broad, even a plurilateral negotiation to talk about excess capacity and how to resolve it."* There have already been **eight months of process and hearings**, and the published rates are upper limits *"often up for negotiation"* — a different instrument from the overnight shock of April 2025. The legal architecture explains the caution. After the **Supreme Court struck down** the emergency basis for the original tariffs earlier this year, the administration is rebuilding on **Section 301** findings and, for over half the Canadian duties, **Section 338** of the 1930s Smoot-Hawley act — two bases, each with its own litigation. **Twenty-five states** have sued to strike down the forced-labour tariffs, and the Senate has a bill to repeal the Section 338 duties. The **Speaker-designate, Hakeem Jeffries**, said the new House would in its first 100 days *"move aggressively to… repeal the Trump tariffs."* China's published **40 per cent** sits above an applied **20 per cent**, the truce rate from last year's Trump–Xi deal, which **expires in mid-January**. Beijing's response was muted: no retaliation, a call to implement the deal, and a dismissal of overcapacity as a *"false issue."* The leverage has weakened with the facts — the US share of China's exports has fallen to around **10 per cent**, from 15 two years ago and 19 in 2018 — and the trade has already rerouted: China's exports in 2026 are down about **8 per cent** on 2024, more than made up for elsewhere, while Canada's exports to the rest of the world have *"rocketed."*

??? warning "Projected Effect (if cause persists)"

    - **Two legal bases mean two independent failure points, and litigation is cheaper than concession** — Section 301 findings, Section 338 duties, 25 states in court, a Senate repeal bill, a House pledged to repeal within 100 days; the direct effect is that a trading partner can now win by doing nothing in Geneva and everything in Washington; the compound consequence is that the schedule's durability rests on American judges and American voters rather than on foreign capitals — an inversion in which the strongest pressure on a US tariff is internal, and the partners who understand it stop negotiating and start funding lawyers
    - **The rerouting has already happened, so the wall now shapes third countries more than the two principals** — China's exports to the US down, its exports everywhere else up by more than the loss; Canada flat to the US and surging elsewhere; the direct effect is that the tariff's measurable cost falls on transshipment hubs, assembly economies and the countries with the least bargaining power; the compound consequence is that the policy intended to return production to America is returning it to Vietnam, Mexico and the fourth name on the list, which is why the fourteenth country receives nothing and the distortion is relocated rather than removed
    - **A legislature that intends to repeal makes every deal signed now time-limited, and partners price expiry** — a Speaker-designate promising repeal in 100 days, a Senate bill, a treaty with a ten-year termination clause already in the news; the direct effect is that counterparties demand ratchets, sunset clauses and renegotiation triggers; the compound consequence is that agreements become longer, more rigid and more legalistic precisely when flexibility is what a rerouted trading system needs — the politics of reversal manufactures the rigidity it complains about
    - **China's silence is a statement about leverage, and the real cliff is mid-January, not the ceiling** — no retaliation, overcapacity dismissed as a false issue, a call to implement the existing deal, a US export share down by a third in two years; the direct effect is that the announced rates are already absorbed; the compound consequence is that the truce expiry is the only date that binds either government, and it falls in the same month as the new duties — so the negotiation that matters is not about the fourteen findings but about whether the truce survives, and the US enters it with less to trade than it had a year ago

    **The hinge:** §4:53 states the criterion in four words — **in mutual consent**. It does not forbid a price; it forbids a price imposed by deception or force, and it places consent, not fairness of rate, at the centre. A tariff is by construction non-consensual, which is why the verse does not settle whether 40 per cent is just. What it does settle is which procedure can make a tariff legitimate: not a finding written by the office that wants the rate, and not a ceiling announced as a fact, but a negotiation in which the other side can say no. **First,** publish **applied and ceiling side by side** for every line, because a ceiling presented as a rate misrepresents the policy and poisons the consultation it invites. **Second,** put the **China truce renewal** into the same plurilateral forum as the overcapacity talks, since the mid-January expiry is the only date that actually binds either government. **Third,** write **durability into every deal** — a ratchet, a sunset, a termination clause — because a partner that cannot rely on the US keeping an agreement will demand terms that make the agreement worth less to both sides, and the cost of that mistrust is paid by the importer and the consumer, who are not in the room.

### 🦠 Twelve Thousand and No Vaccine

???+ danger "Observed Cause"
    **The Ebola outbreak in eastern Congo passed twelve thousand confirmed cases on Thursday, and the number that matters more is the one the response cannot reach.** Congo's health ministry reported **12,021 confirmed cases**; nearly half of those infected have died. It is the **fastest-growing Ebola outbreak in history**, the deadliest of Congo's **17** outbreaks, and on track to surpass the West African epidemic that killed **11,000** people between 2014 and 2016. The outbreak was declared in **May** and is caused by the rare **Bundibugyo virus**, for which there is **no approved vaccine or treatment** — which means the entire response consists of isolation, contact tracing and safe burial, three interventions that require reaching people. On Thursday morning fighting resumed between the army and the **Convention pour la révolution populaire** (CRP) in **Iga-Barrière**, a mining town about **25 kilometres north of Bunia**, sending new waves of displaced people toward the city. Bunia, the capital of **Ituri** province, is the principal hub for the Ebola response. The CRP is one of more than **100** armed groups in eastern Congo and was founded last year by **Thomas Lubanga**, convicted by the International Criminal Court in 2012 of recruiting children under 15. **Dr Catherine Smallwood**, the WHO's Ebola incident manager, said this week's fighting had already prevented teams from reaching some communities, disrupted monitoring of exposed contacts and left treatment centres with fewer staff; about **a dozen people**, including **two WHO personnel** and Congolese health officials, were evacuated to Bunia on Tuesday after **three days trapped in a hospital in Akwe** amid intense crossfire. Response teams are also contending with poor roads and violence from residents driven by **mistrust and misinformation**.

??? warning "Projected Effect (if cause persists)"
    - **With no vaccine, the response is logistics, and logistics in a war zone is a security problem** — Bundibugyo has no licensed countermeasure, so the tools are isolation, tracing and burial; the direct effect is that every road closure is an epidemiological event; the compound consequence is that the outbreak's growth rate is now a function of the front line rather than of the virus, which means the epidemic will expand and contract with the fighting and no medical agency can control either
    - **A case fatality near one in two will not fall without supportive care, and the treatment centre is the thing everyone fears** — nearly half of 12,021 dead, no specific therapy, care depending on fluids, isolation and staffing; the direct effect is that survival depends on reaching a centre early; the compound consequence is that the centre is also where the disease concentrates and where the rumours attach, so the intervention that saves lives carries the most risk and the least trust — and trust is not a supply that can be airlifted
    - **Evacuating international staff sets a precedent that the next deployment reads as a signal** — a dozen evacuated after three days in a hospital under crossfire; the direct effect is that the Akwe facility is lost as a treatment point; the compound consequence is that the next organisation deciding whether to send teams to Ituri will remember that the WHO pulled its people out, and a response that contracts at the moment it must expand loses the contacts, the local knowledge and the community relationships that took months to build
    - **Displacement exports the outbreak, and the export is declared after it happens** — new waves of displaced people moving toward Bunia, a province bordering Uganda and South Sudan, surveillance already disrupted; the direct effect is that cases appear outside the monitored zone; the compound consequence is that a cross-border emergency will be recognised only when a neighbouring country confirms a case, at which point the response becomes a regional operation with borders, politics and delays that the virus does not observe
    - **Surpassing the West African toll will change the frame from outbreak to failure, and the accounting will move to access** — 11,000 deaths in 2014–16 as the benchmark, a trajectory that passes it within weeks; the direct effect is that this becomes the largest Ebola epidemic on record; the compound consequence is that the question shifts from virology to permission — who blocked the corridor, who refused the burial team, which armed group held the road — and the answers will be demanded from institutions that spent the year describing the outbreak as fast-moving rather than as unreachable

    *"And care for the sick and watch out for their healing, and offer consolation to those in mourning, and be helpful to them in all things that they need."* — *Goblet of the Truth*, Chapter 3, Verse 16

    **The hinge:** §3:16 is short, and it contains no exception for insecurity. The duty is to care for the sick, watch for their healing, console the mourners and be helpful *"in all things that they need"* — and in Ituri the thing they need is **access**. That makes the verse a logistical instruction as much as a moral one: a responder who cannot reach the patient has not failed at medicine, and the obligation therefore falls on whoever controls the road. **First,** negotiate a **published humanitarian corridor** with the CRP and the army at command level, with a named liaison on each side and a standing notification procedure — this is not a political concession to armed groups, it is the medical intervention, and it is the only one that scales. **Second,** **pay and protect local responders** instead of evacuating international staff: the Congolese health worker stays, speaks the language and is the only continuous presence the community has, and a hazard allowance plus insurance is cheaper than a redeployment. **Third,** fund **safe-burial teams** as a separate line from treatment, because burial is the transmission event around which mistrust crystallises and the one intervention that works without a vaccine, a laboratory or a stable front line.

### 🌍 Seven Thousand Tonnes

???+ danger "Observed Cause"
    **A tanker carrying up to seven thousand tonnes of diesel capsized twenty-five nautical miles off Ibiza in the early hours of Thursday, and by Friday morning nobody could say what was in the water.** The **Med Emre**, a **Malta-flagged** chemical and products tanker, was sailing from **Algeciras** to **Barcelona** when rescuers in Valencia received a distress call at about **22:30 local time on Wednesday**. Minutes later the vessel reported a leak, and the crew said it was leaning at **90 degrees**. All **17 crew** were rescued, but **seven** were taken to hospital and one was later pronounced dead; the six receiving treatment reportedly had minor injuries including hypothermia. The ship did not sink completely, as first reported — it remains **partially submerged**, 25 nautical miles (46 km) from Ibiza and 57 from Valencia, with the cause of the capsizing still unclear. Spain sent the pollution-control vessel **Clara Campoamor** to assess the environmental impact. The Balearic president, **Marga Prohens**, wrote that *"there is still little information available about a possible fuel spill"* and that *"the situation is stabilised"*, with further inspections due on Friday morning. The water it is sitting in is not ordinary water: **Ibiza's marine environment was added to the UNESCO World Heritage List in 1999**, and the tanker is carrying a cargo that disperses rather than congeals — which makes the next 48 hours, and the weather, the decisive variables.

??? warning "Projected Effect (if cause persists)"
    - **Diesel is not heavy fuel, and the difference changes both the damage and the politics** — up to 7,000 tonnes of a light product that evaporates and disperses, in a semi-enclosed sea; the direct effect is that the acute toxicity is concentrated in the water column rather than on the shoreline; the compound consequence is that a dispersed spill is harder to photograph, easier to under-report and quicker to leave the headlines — so political pressure decays faster than the contamination, and the monitoring that matters most is the monitoring nobody will fund after the cameras leave
    - **A partially submerged wreck is an unsealed source, and the salvage decision belongs to people the coastline cannot vote for** — the vessel on its side, cargo still aboard, inspections pending; the direct effect is that the source can shift, break up or sink at any point in the next weeks; the compound consequence is that tow, seal or lighter is chosen by the owner, the insurer and the flag state, and the cheapest option is often to let a damaged ship go down in deep water — which converts a surface spill into a seabed one and a visible problem into an invisible liability
    - **The flag is the instrument that separates the risk from the responsibility** — a Maltese flag, Spanish waters, Spanish ports at both ends, a crew of seventeen whose nationality has not been central to the story; the direct effect is that liability runs through a flag state, an owner, a protection-and-indemnity club and a charterer; the compound consequence is that the coast that bears the damage has no authority over any of them, so the cost of a bad ship is externalised onto a tourism economy and a marine reserve — and the incentive to run an old hull under a convenient flag is exactly the incentive the structure creates
    - **A visible sheen costs more than the ecological damage, which rewards delay** — an island economy entering its planning season, a UNESCO site, an official statement that the situation is stabilised before the spill is characterised; the direct effect is that bookings react to images, not to concentrations; the compound consequence is that the responsible communicator is punished for saying *"we do not know yet"*, so the incentive runs toward a reassuring sentence now and a correction later — and the correction never gets the coverage the reassurance did
    - **This is a sample, not an accident, and the fleet is the cause** — one tanker on one of the busiest routes in the Mediterranean, a sea that carries a large share of Europe's refined products; the direct effect is that the same hulls, flags, crewing levels and inspection intervals are in use on hundreds of sister routes; the compound consequence is that nothing about this event is unusual except that it happened near a UNESCO site, so the regulatory response will be calibrated to the visibility of the coast rather than to the condition of the fleet

    *"…so you make your home planet into a shadow world (hell) and place of existence for a limited time, because you wreak destruction on the appearance (nature) and destroy your world through battles (wars) and hatred as well as through jealousy, through pathological craving for revenge and pathological craving for retaliation and through everything evil."* — *Goblet of the Truth*, Chapter 7, Verse 25

    **The hinge:** §7:25 locates the cause of a shadow world in **destruction of the appearance of nature** — not in malice, and not in a single catastrophe, but in accumulated practice. That is the correct frame for a capsized tanker: the destruction, if it comes, will have been decided years earlier, in a flag registry, an inspection interval, a crewing level and a freight rate. The verse therefore does not ask what caused this hull to turn over. It asks what makes a fleet like this one economical. **First,** publish the **inspection record, age and classification history** of the Med Emre within the week, because causation will be argued by insurers and the record is the only evidence that can be checked before the file is settled. **Second,** keep a **standing salvage and lightering capability** in the Balearics — booms, pumps and a vessel on contract, not on call — because the first 48 hours are decided by what is already on the water, and a pollution-control ship arriving to *"assess"* is an observer, not a response. **Third,** attach **routing and reporting requirements for laden tankers** in the Balearic corridor, with a position report and a casualty notification duty, because a sea that carries this much refined product past a World Heritage site should know what is moving through it before something turns over.

## The Pattern

Read the five together — **a maintenance contract in a live war zone**, **a tariff ceiling presented as a rate**, **four portfolios bought with a veto attached**, **an outbreak measured by the roads it cannot reach**, **seven thousand tonnes under a Maltese flag** — and the common structure is **the instrument**. Each harm today passed through a document or a designation that placed responsibility somewhere other than where the decision was made: an employment contract that made a technician a civilian, a Federal Register notice that called a ceiling a duty, a coalition agreement that pays in ministries, an access negotiation that never happened, a ship's registration that separates the flag from the coast.

**The instrument is now being tested in all five.** Kuwait and Rome are arguing about whether the dead were civilians. Twenty-five American states and a new House are arguing about whether the tariff has a legal basis at all. A Haredi party is arguing that a coalition clause can override an appointment the Basic Laws assign to the government. The WHO is arguing, in effect, that a hospital in Akwe is a medical facility and not a military objective, and losing the argument to crossfire. Spain is waiting for Friday's inspection to learn whether the hull it is looking at is a wreck or a source. In each case the test is identical: **does the instrument allocate the risk to the party that can control it?** A contract that puts the risk on the technician, a tariff that puts it on the importer, a coalition clause that puts it on the appointee, an access regime that puts it on the patient, a flag that puts it on the coastline — each is a decision to move the cost away from the decision-maker, and each is now visible enough to be contested.

**And the cost still lands where the decision is not.** The families of two men who were servicing a radar at two in the afternoon. The importer who pays the applied rate and the consumer who pays the importer. The Israeli voter who will be handed a coalition contract and a ceasefire it may not survive, and the family in Gaza who will not be handed either. The patient twenty-five kilometres north of Bunia, on a road that a militia closed on Thursday morning. The crew of seventeen and the island economy that will price the sheen before it prices the seagrass. None of them appear in the documents that now decide what happens to them.

In every case, the human cause is **a choice** — and therefore the effect is **changeable**. Note what moved in twenty-four hours: a drone that was supposed to be intercepted killed the people maintaining the interception, which converts a defence contract into a legal question; a tariff that was announced as a policy was described by its own architect as the opening of a negotiation, which converts a rate into a deadline; a coalition that was stalled on Tuesday was signed on Thursday at the price of four ministries and a veto it cannot honour; an outbreak that was a medical emergency became a question about a road; and a ship that was a casualty became an inspection. Five things resolve in the coming weeks: **the drone's technical chain**, where a serial number and a flight path are the difference between a finding and an accusation; **the applied rate and the China truce on 15 January**, which is the only date in the tariff file that either government must meet; **Israel's 6 December**, where a published contract is the only thing that can make a four-portfolio government legitimate; **the corridor to Iga-Barrière**, which is a negotiation with an armed group and therefore the most direct medical intervention available; and **the first hull inspection and cargo survey off Ibiza**, which is the last moment at which the cause can be established before the sea and the insurers settle it. Each is a place where an instrument that already exists could be made to allocate risk honestly — and which of them is opened to inspection is the only part of any of this that is still ours.
