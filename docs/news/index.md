The law of cause and effect is the most precise and impartial of all creational laws. It does not punish or reward — **it reflects**. Every thought, feeling, word, and deed generates a specific vibrational cause. That cause accumulates energy, compresses into lived experience, and returns — amplified — as your destiny.

On this page, we track **current world events as observed causes** and project their **necessary effects** if those causes persist unchanged. This is not fortune-telling. It is **causal inference** — the same principle that lets a physicist predict a trajectory from position and velocity. See the cause, know the effect. Change the cause, and the effect dissolves.

### 🔥 The Coordination Without a Trigger

???+ danger "Observed Cause"
    **Four days after Russian objects came down on Polish soil, Warsaw's answer was to deepen the technical relationship rather than to name a threshold for fire.** On **Tuesday 24 November** at the **Pentagon**, Poland's deputy defence minister **Cezary Tomczyk** and US under secretary of war for research and engineering **Emil Michael** signed a defence research, development, testing and evaluation agreement that had been under negotiation for what Tomczyk called a *"probably record-breaking"* **17 years**, giving Poland a formal role in developing American defence technology for the first time. **General Marcin Górka**, who oversees innovation in the Polish armed forces and travelled with the delegation, said the deal *"opens the doors to American military laboratories for our scientists and engineers"* and would let the two countries jointly develop **anti-drone systems and advanced drones**, tested in both countries. Tomczyk used the same Washington briefing to call the missile incursions a Russian **"provocation"** and, asked about President **Trump** having described **Vladimir Putin** as a friend, replied: *"one thing is certain: Vladimir Putin is not our friend; he is a threat to us."* Separately, **NATO approved the deployment of an Airborne Warning and Control System to Poland** in response to the three Russian missiles that struck Polish territory last week, and **Bloomberg** reported on Wednesday that Warsaw is now formally asking the alliance to **shoot down Russian missiles over Ukraine** — a request that, if granted, moves the interception line from Poland's border into Ukraine's airspace and makes every NATO aircraft over the region a party to the engagement decision. The American signals point in opposite directions: the **Washington Post** reported that Trump called the incursion a **"mistake"**, while the **Financial Times** reported that roughly **60 Patriot interceptors** paid for by allies remain held pending a re-export approval by Defence Secretary **Pete Hegseth**, and **TVP World** reported that **bad weather** prevented a shoot-down on the night of the first incursions. The **Kyiv Post** reported that the **Kh-101** recovered in Poland was manufactured in **late 2026** — weeks, not years, before it crossed the border.

??? warning "Projected Effect (if cause persists)"

    - **One ally calling the incursion a "mistake" while another calls it a "provocation" is not a disagreement about facts, it is a disagreement about what the facts cost** — Trump's word and Tomczyk's word describe the same flight path; the direct effect is that the deterrent signal is split, emitted from two capitals in two directions; the compound consequence is that Moscow can price each capital separately, and the cheapest route to weakening the guarantee is to test the one whose elected leader prefers the word "mistake"

### 🇺🇳 The Fourteen Jurisdictions

???+ danger "Observed Cause"
    **The United States is preparing to announce new tariffs on at least fourteen jurisdictions, possibly as early as Wednesday, with China, the European Union and Japan among those affected.** The BBC understands the announcement is being prepared for this week, that the rates will differ by country according to the deals each has already struck with President **Trump**, and that the new import taxes would hit many of America's largest trading partners. The stated rationale is the one **US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer** advanced for the earlier round — that overproduction abroad is displacing American manufacturing and deterring investment — a round that was **later struck down**, which is why the administration is rebuilding the same structure deal by deal rather than in a single instrument. The timing is not incidental. The two sides' interim agreement **expires on 10 January**, and despite last week's **Trump–Xi meeting** at the Shenzhen summit the dispute is unresolved: Washington has accused Beijing of falling short on what it agreed in **October 2025**, *"particularly as they relate to agricultural purchases, rare earths, and other critical minerals."* Beijing's counter-move came on Tuesday, when China **extended the suspension of its export ban on gallium, germanium and antimony until 27 January** — a ban that would otherwise have taken effect on **Friday**. Both sides are therefore holding a deadline they can tighten at will, and both have tightened it into the same six-week window. The domestic cost is not theoretical either: the tariffs have been criticised for raising prices for American consumers and damaging the global economy, and this round arrives with the previous round's legal basis already removed.

??? warning "Projected Effect (if cause persists)"
    - **A tariff schedule differentiated by bilateral deal converts trade policy into a loyalty price list, and the list is published** — fourteen-plus jurisdictions, rates set by what each has conceded; the direct effect is that every government can read the price of its own alignment; the compound consequence is that the tariff stops being an economic instrument and becomes a membership fee, and a fee that is visible is a fee that gets negotiated — so the next year is spent in bilateral rooms rather than in the rules-based system the tariffs were nominally defending
    - **The consumer pays at the port, not at the podium, and the invoice arrives before the factory does** — a tax on imports is collected from the importer and passed forward; the direct effect is higher shelf prices within one shipping cycle; the compound consequence is that the reshored plant the policy is meant to produce takes years to build while the price increase takes weeks, so the political credit and the political cost sit on different clocks and the cost's clock runs first
    - **Two export-control suspensions expiring six weeks apart is a countdown, not a negotiation** — the US interim agreement to 10 January, China's minerals suspension to 27 January, gallium, germanium and antimony named explicitly; the direct effect is that both sides retain a switch; the compound consequence is that the deadline itself becomes the leverage, so each side's best move is to wait until the other's clock is closer to zero — a design that produces crisis by construction, and the crisis lands on the manufacturers in third countries who buy the minerals and are party to neither agreement
    - **A tariff struck down and re-issued country by country tells every trading partner that the constraint was legal, not political** — the earlier round failed in the courts and returns as a set of bilateral deals; the direct effect is that the policy survives review by changing its form; the compound consequence is that the durability of any concession is now in doubt on both sides, so a partner asked to commit for a decade prices in the chance that the whole architecture is annulled again — and commits less, for shorter, at a higher price

    *"Consider, each one is given everything according to his or her merits, and so it shall also be in a bond (contract) if you have concluded one with one another so that one party shall not be disadvantaged whilst the other is advantaged (receives a benefit), therefore everything shall always be regulated so that both parties receive the same shares and rights such that one does not have more than the other; and if you conclude a bond (contract) for a deal, then it shall be such that the one who is the grantor (seller) does not give excessive advantage to the receiver (purchaser) and the latter does not carry out his or her obligation to (towards) the grantor (seller) tardily (does not discharge the responsibility)."* — *Goblet of the Truth*, Chapter 4, Verse 57

    **The hinge:** §4:57 states the test for a contract in one clause — *"one party shall not be disadvantaged whilst the other is advantaged"* — and then adds what makes it enforceable: both parties must discharge their obligations, neither late nor grudgingly. A tariff differentiated by prior concessions fails the first clause by construction, because its purpose is to advantage one party; an interim agreement allowed to run into a deadline fails the second. The verse does not forbid protecting an industry; it forbids calling a leverage schedule a contract. **First,** state the **criteria** for each rate — what a jurisdiction must do to move from one tier to another — because a price without a published condition can only be paid, never earned. **Second,** decouple **critical minerals from the tariff clock**: a six-week countdown on gallium and germanium is a countdown on every electronics supply chain that is neither American nor Chinese. **Third,** put the **renewal of the interim agreement** on a fixed date with a published text, so that 10 January is a decision that either happens or is formally deferred, rather than a cliff both sides approach while describing the other as the one walking toward it.

### 🤖 Ten of Twelve

???+ danger "Observed Cause"
    **A Brooklyn jury convicted Huawei of stealing American trade secrets, and declined to convict it of being a criminal enterprise.** After a **two-month trial**, jurors found the Chinese telecommunications giant **guilty on 10 of 12 counts**, including **conspiracy to steal trade secrets**, **bank fraud** and **conspiracy to violate US sanctions** — but **not guilty of the racketeering conspiracy charge**, which required them to find the firm had operated as a criminal organisation. Prosecutors alleged that Huawei spent two decades lying, cheating and stealing for competitive advantage: assistant US attorney **Meredith Arfa** told the jury in closing that the company lied to banks about its business dealings in **Iran** and stole technology secrets from rival firms *"to ensure it had a competitive edge."* The defence, led by **Brian Heberlig**, argued that prosecutors decided Huawei was a criminal organisation and built the case backwards from that conclusion, and that its reach — operations in **170 countries**, more than **200,000 staff** — was the product of hard work. After the verdict Heberlig told the BBC the firm was *"gratified that the jury had rejected the racketeering charge"*, calling the RICO count *"a significant overreach"*, while conceding *"mixed emotions about the rest of the verdict."* The **Chinese government** called the case US *"suppression"* of Chinese companies and said it supports firms defending their interests. **Sentencing is set for September 2027**, and Huawei's counsel said there will be *"significant advocacy"* for the lowest possible sentence and, after it, an appeal — *"that fight will continue and the company's not going to rest until it's pursued every last argument it can through appeal."*

??? warning "Projected Effect (if cause persists)"
    - **A verdict that separates the act from the enterprise sets the price of each** — ten counts of theft, fraud and sanctions conspiracy, no finding of organised crime; the direct effect is that the conduct is punished while the structure is not named; the compound consequence is that the most consequential legal question — whether a firm's practices amount to a policy — has now been answered in the negative by a jury that saw two months of evidence, and that answer will be cited in every future case against every large company as proof that scale is not intent
    - **A sentencing date nineteen months away is a negotiation with a docket, and the appeal begins before the sentence** — September 2027, with counsel already promising to contest the result; the direct effect is that no penalty attaches while the conduct continues to be litigated; the compound consequence is that the deterrent value of a conviction decays in the interval, and a competitor watching learns that the cost of being found guilty is a decade of filings rather than a penalty
    - **The sanctions count is the one that travels, because bank fraud is a passport issue** — conspiracy to violate US sanctions and to defraud banks touches the dollar-clearing system every institution depends on; the direct effect is that Huawei's banking relationships are now a legal exposure for its counterparties; the compound consequence is that the verdict exports itself — no US prosecutor is needed for a European or Asian bank to decide that a convicted counterparty is not worth the compliance file, which is how a single verdict becomes a global constraint
    - **"Suppression" as the official response converts a courtroom finding into a geopolitical grievance, and the grievance outlives the case** — Beijing's framing, a promised appeal, a tariff schedule announced the same week; the direct effect is that the verdict is absorbed into the same dispute as the minerals and the tariffs; the compound consequence is that no Chinese firm can now settle such a case without being seen to concede to suppression, so every future prosecution becomes a state-to-state event and the ordinary remedy — admit, pay, reform — is taken off the table by politics

    *"Do not split up (share) your possessions amongst one another with illicit (forbidden) means (deception/theft); obtain (earn) everything with honest commerce and in mutual consent."* — *Goblet of the Truth*, Chapter 4, Verse 53

    **The hinge:** §4:53 is one sentence and holds both halves of the standard — the means must be honest **and** the consent mutual. A trade secret taken is deception; a bank told a false purpose gave a consent it never gave. Note what the verse does *not* say: it does not say that size is guilt, and a jury hearing the same distinction put to it in the language of RICO refused to make that leap. That refusal is the hinge's evidence — the line between a company that cheats and a company that cheats *by design* is a real line, and it is drawable. **First,** publish the **remedy**, not merely the verdict: what Huawei must change in its compliance structure for the sentence to be short, because a penalty without a specified repair teaches nothing. **Second,** keep the **appeal from becoming the argument** — a conviction contested on procedure is legitimate; a conviction contested as foreign policy is a different proceeding in a different forum, and mixing them costs both. **Third,** apply the **same test to the counterparties** — Megaspeed, the banks, the distributors — because a standard enforced only against the largest defendant is a price list for everyone else.

### ☣️ Sixty-Eight Barrels

???+ danger "Observed Cause"
    **On Wednesday morning, the day before Thanksgiving, the US Army intends to detonate 68 barrels — about five tonnes, some 10,000 pounds — of lead azide at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant in Middletown, Iowa, and the people downwind have been left to decide for themselves whether to leave.** Lead azide is a highly volatile detonator compound that has been deteriorating underground, and the Army's case is that a controlled blast is safer than the spontaneous one that becomes likelier as temperatures fall in the coming days: *"there is consensus across the experts across the country that a controlled, mitigated detonation of the lead azide here at Iowa Army Ammunition Plant is far preferable to an uncontrolled spontaneous detonation of that substance."* The detonation proceeds because an **appeals court overturned** an earlier order stopping it over the health and environmental hazards of lead exposure. Officials have announced a **five-mile optional evacuation zone** and a **20-mile shelter-in-place advisory**, with airborne lead dust potentially reaching **up to 100 miles** depending on wind — and residents who stay have been told to expect *"temporary noise, ground vibrations, and a dust plume that will contain some lead."* A federal health official, **Sean Kaufman** of the Administration for Strategic Preparedness and Response, was blunter: *"if they are women who are pregnant, children under the age of 6, or children in general, and individuals who may have chronic disease, you definitely want to be out of that area. I would evacuate that area."* The **New York Times** reports that the Army **rejected a cleaner option** for disposing of the material. Many communities learned late: **Fort Madison** city manager **Laura Leigois**, roughly 20 miles away, said *"we were blindsided"* — the city was first notified on **17 November** about a detonation originally planned three days later. **Margo Sprenger**, 71, a farmer and landowner in Donnelson about 20 miles south-west of the plant, said her land lies in the direct path of the projected plume: *"our grandparents started on these farms, and now we don't even know if they can produce food that we can trust… we certainly know the dangers of lead, but we don't know the dangers of what they're doing to us."* In Middletown itself, population around 360, the mood is different — resident **Trisha Klaus** told the BBC *"I actually want it to happen, because I'd rather it be controlled than just blow up"* — and Mayor **Rodney Johnson** noted that crop dusters already fly over the town every two weeks.

??? warning "Projected Effect (if cause persists)"
    - **A plume is a decision executed by the weather, which means the people who made it never have to name its recipients** — a five-mile optional evacuation, a twenty-mile shelter advisory, dust potentially to a hundred miles; the direct effect is that exposure becomes a function of wind direction; the compound consequence is that no list of affected households exists before the blast and none will exist after it, so the harm is real, distributed and unassignable — and an unassignable harm cannot be compensated, only denied
    - **"Optional evacuation" transfers the state's risk calculation to the household with the least information** — pregnant women, children under six and the chronically ill advised by a federal official to leave, with no mandatory order and no funded relocation; the direct effect is that those who can afford to go, go; the compound consequence is that those who stay are precisely those without the means to leave, which converts a public-health measure into a means test and leaves the poorest holding the residual risk of a decision taken in Washington
    - **Rejecting the cleaner option converts a disposal problem into a dispersal problem, and the difference is permanent** — lead that is contained can be monitored; lead lofted into soil and pasture cannot be recalled; the direct effect is that contamination enters the food chain at the level of the farm rather than the plant; the compound consequence is that liability shifts from the Army's remediation budget to individual farmers who must now prove at their own cost whether their land and produce are safe — and a farmer who cannot afford the test cannot sell the crop, so the market performs the condemnation the regulators declined to
    - **An appeals court reversing a stop is a legal event that reads, on the ground, as the last door closing** — the blast proceeds on the finding that the alternative is worse, which may well be true; the direct effect is that the decision is now irreversible; the compound consequence is that the only question left for the public is "was it safe?", which will be answered by the same agencies that chose the method — and trust, once the sequencing is this visible, is not recovered by a press release after the dust settles
    - **The Army is not wrong that an uncontrolled detonation would be worse; that is exactly why the failure is upstream** — deteriorating lead azide stored in the ground for years, a disposal decision deferred until the weather forced it, an evacuation plan drafted after the maps were published; the direct effect is a defensible emergency action; the compound consequence is that every future emergency of this kind will be defended the same way, so the standard becomes "we had no choice" — and a standard of no choice is a standing licence to arrive unprepared

    *"If you heed yourselves inwardly, then you will be warned through your admonishment (conscience) against all inequitableness and against all unfairness (irresponsibility), as well as against all unrighteousness, so that you can then decide what you must do and what you must leave undone."* — *Goblet of the Truth*, Chapter 19, Verse 23

    **The hinge:** §19:23 gives the test as a sequence: heed inward, be warned, then *decide what you must do and what you must leave undone*. Iowa's sequence ran backwards — the method was chosen, the plume maps were published, and the warning reached the public afterwards as a leaflet with an evacuation radius. The verse does not say the detonation is wrong; the Army's reasoning may be sound. It says the warning must come **before** the decision, and that a decision taken without it is not a decision but a reflex. **First,** make the health protection **mandatory and funded** inside the plume path — temporary relocation paid for, not advised, because advice to evacuate that costs a family money is advice only the solvent can take. **Second,** publish the **baseline soil and water data** for the affected counties *before* the blast, so that any later contamination can be attributed rather than argued about for a decade. **Third,** answer the question Sprenger actually asked — *"who's going to tell us if it is safe or not?"* — by funding independent testing of the farmland and the produce, at public expense, with the results published, because a farmer who must pay to prove his own land is clean has been fined for the Army's choice.

### 🏛️ The Pardon That Was Already an Option

    - **A security clearance granted around its beneficiary is the part that cannot be briefed away** — intelligence found no evidence of espionage, which is a finding about conduct; a clearance is a judgement about risk, and it was granted in a process the beneficiary's patron influenced; the direct effect is that the man appearing before Congress holds access he obtained controversially; the compound consequence is that every allied service that shares material with US counterparts must now price in a clearance decision taken under political pressure — and the ones who share most will quietly share less, a cost paid in capability rather than in headlines
    - **The company's fate and the president's are now one instrument, so a market decline is a political event and a political favour is a market event** — a 7 per cent fall on congressional demands, a trillion-dollar rise on a phone call, an AI-generated image posted as the seal of the arrangement; the direct effect is extreme volatility in the most systemically important stock in the index; the compound consequence is that any future adverse finding about Huang transmits directly into both the equity and the presidency, so neither side has an incentive to resolve the matter — only to keep it unresolved and survivable, which is the one outcome in which nobody is positioned to want the truth

    **The hinge:** §2:246 names the exact transaction now under scrutiny — wealth offered *"to authority in order to be granted advantages and right while in the unright"* — and supplies the test that separates it from ordinary commerce: was the advantage obtained knowingly in the unright? ## The Pattern

Read the five together — **a defence that co-ordinates but does not fire**, **a tariff list priced by loyalty**, **a verdict that finds the theft but not the enterprise**, **a detonation whose warning arrived after its method**, **a pardon offered before a charge** — and the common structure is not incompetence. It is that **in each case the correct instrument exists, and the decision that would make it act has been moved to a place where it cannot be examined.**

**The instruments are all present and working.** Poland has Patriots powered, AWACS now approved, fighters airborne and a brand-new agreement opening American laboratories to its engineers — and in four days nothing has been intercepted, because the missing item is a written rule, not a missile. The United States has a trade representative, a statutory process and fourteen negotiating relationships, and is announcing rates by country rather than publishing criteria. A federal jury sat for two months, heard the evidence and returned ten convictions — and declined the one finding that would have named the enterprise, leaving a verdict that punishes conduct while certifying the structure. The Army has experts, a court order, an evacuation plan and a plume model, and published the model after the method was chosen. The Justice Department has an attorney general, an FBI and a clearance procedure, and the man at the centre of it has been promised a pardon before he has been charged.

**In every case the decision has been relocated to where scrutiny cannot follow it.** Firing a missile becomes a weather question and a signature. A tariff becomes a bilateral concession made in a room without minutes. Corporate intent becomes a RICO count a jury will not stretch to. A disposal method becomes an appeals-court docket. A prosecution becomes a pardon that is *"always an option."* Each relocation is procedurally impeccable — which is the point, because a decision that is procedurally impeccable cannot be criticised as improper, only as unfortunate.

**And the cost lands where the decision is not.** The household under the flight path in Podkarpackie. The consumer at the port paying a loyalty price within one shipping cycle. The European bank that must now price a convicted counterparty. The farmer in Donnelson who cannot afford to prove her own soil is clean. The allied service that will share a little less, and the shareholder holding the stock whose chief executive's fate has become a political instrument. None of them sat in the rooms where this week's decisions were taken, and all of them live inside the results.
