## The Pattern

Look across these five stories. Though they span **twenty Houthi attacks on Riyadh's airport since 1 October, 19 dead and 364 wounded, as Egypt's parliament is asked to authorise forces and Sanaa warns Cairo off**; **Romanian F-16s downing Russian jet drones over their own waters while Kyiv says Washington has capped its satellite link**; **1,800 arrests and more than 500 injured officers in France as Macron speaks of "anger and shame" and the National Rally moves into position**; **ten arrests for the olive-harvest attacks while ministers keep questioning the facts**; and **a trillion-dollar gold trade whose clean-versus-dirty line is an industry fiction** — the common structure is unmistakable.
