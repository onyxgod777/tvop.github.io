Witness protection that makes naming a killer survivable; weapons-supply enforcement treated as the upstream cause; and the trafficking and illegal-mining economy confronted as the economic question it is: each is a decision available to the state, the communities and the economy alike, and each changes the cause without waiting for the next funeral.

## The Pattern

Look across these five stories. Though they span **a war in which a refusal was answered by a demand for a formal channel while the war's defence came to an allied air base in three white trucks, a bomb robot and a 400-metre cordon**; **a second Ethiopian war in which Tigray-aligned fighters took three Afar towns while the command announced *"strategic calm"* and a monitor logged the fighting the official accounts omitted**; **a week in which Bangkok priced its flood at $320 million and the north-east US counted its first death as India and Nepal counted seventy more**; **a Serbian president who resigned the office he was barred from renewing to contest the one he could hold, after pardoning 49 on the way out**; and **a country where twin shootings rose to twenty-eight dead as police named robbery and could not rule out the mines** — the common structure is unmistakable.

**In every one of the five, the event described as news is the *effect*. The cause is upstream, human, and named: a decision to keep a base flying strike missions while leaving its host neighbourhoods to a cordon, and to leave a signed document unanswered on the record while the tempo of the war is set by a domestic vote; a decision to answer a widening war with a stated posture rather than a mechanism, and to impose a blackout over the incidents that would decide it; a decision to rebuild the lowest districts of two hemispheres' cities to a standard the last comparable event already disproved, and to book the loss as the price of rain; a decision to meet a constitutional limit on one office by seeking another, and to settle the accounts of the period under judgement before the vote; and a decision to leave a homicide rate, a weapons supply and the shadow economies under both untouched while investigating each mass event.** 

In every case, the cause is **a human choice** — and therefore, the effect is **changeable**.
