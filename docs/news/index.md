### 💶 Merz and Macron Move to Give Brussels a "Trade Bazooka": a Joint Push to Cut China Off From the Single Market

???+ danger "Observed Cause"
    Two of Europe's biggest economies have moved to arm the European Commission for economic war, days before a critical negotiation. **On Monday 5 October 2026, German Chancellor Friedrich Merz and French President Emmanuel Macron sent a joint letter and position paper to European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen urging the EU to adopt new powers that could restrict — or "completely cut off" — imports from countries seen as creating "severe and systematic distortions" in the single market, calling the EU's existing trade-defence toolbox insufficient** (Politico, Bloomberg, France 24, Agence Europe, SCMP). **Neither leader named China, but the target is unmistakable: the EU's goods deficit with China reached roughly €360 billion (about $400 billion) last year, and Brussels accuses Chinese overcapacity, subsidies and currency practices of "systemic market-distorting practices" that damage key European industries; the letter landed just days before EU trade commissioner Maroš Šefčovič flies to Beijing for the final round of a four-month push to rebalance the relationship** (Politico, France 24, Reuters/Nikkei Asia, Agence Europe). **The proposed instrument would let the Commission act "within days" — far faster than the existing Anti-Coercion Instrument (the EU's trade "bazooka"), which in practice requires months of investigation and a qualified majority of member states to activate; under the new design, Commission measures would stand unless a "reverse" qualified majority of capitals voted to block them** (Politico, Agence Europe). **Berlin and Paris also want a second instrument to limit single-market access, a diversification instrument to reduce dependency on Chinese imports and critical raw materials, the use of "ex officio" investigations without waiting for a complaint, and greater use of existing tools; German officials said the EU needs a weapon as powerful as the United States' Section 301 tariffs, and the Commission welcomed the paper ahead of an EU leaders' summit on 15–16 October** (Nikkei, Politico, Agence Europe, France 24).

??? warning "Projected Effect (if cause persists)"

    - **The consumer and the taxpayer pay the invoice, and never see the bill** — the direct effect of restricting cheap imports is that prices rise for households and inputs for industry; the compound consequence is that the cost of the confrontation is dispersed across millions of people who chose none of it, while the decisions are taken in a handful of capitals and announced as protection — so the politics of the trade war is fought over who is blamed for the higher price

    - **But an accident is a warning, not a sentence** — the moment the details are shared openly enough for contacts to be traced and prophylaxis given; the moment the same diligence is applied to the laboratories of every state, in peace and in war; and the moment those who hold dangerous pathogens account for them as a duty owed to all humankind, the incident can be closed at quarantine. §122 states the rule that every such institution is bound by: you are responsible for all and everything you do.
