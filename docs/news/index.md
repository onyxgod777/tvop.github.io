## The Pattern

Read the five together — **a warship sunk in a third country's port**, **the longest climate summit in UN history ending without a text**, **a prime minister pressing a strike his own intelligence service says will open several fronts**, **the first genocide judgment ever issued by the World Court**, and **a Brexit settlement whose costs begin in 2028** — and the common structure is not failure, and not hypocrisy. It is that **each of these five fights is being conducted over a word, because the word decides which regime applies.**

**In each case the substance was not negotiated; the classification was, and the classification is the substance.** Benghazi turns on whether a corvette refuelling in a third state's harbour is a *legitimate target* — because if it is, the war has no jurisdiction boundary, and if it is not, seven drones were an act of war in conscious commission. Antalya turned on the words *fossil fuels* — six in Monday's draft, zero on Sunday — because a text that names the cause obliges the states that produce it. Jerusalem turns on *localized event* against *multiple fronts* — a three-day operation sells, three fronts do not, and the same strike is either one thing or the other depending on which estimate you accept. The Hague turned on *intent* — because killing, harm and birth-prevention become genocide only when the only reasonable inference is the destruction of a group, and that inference is where a court's entire authority is spent. London turns on *alignment* — because "following some EU regulations" is either a technical schedule or a constitutional surrender, and nobody has said which in public.

**And the decision always migrates to the place where verification is weakest.** A consensus plenary where one producer's objection is total. A closed security cabinet where the assessment is delivered orally and discounted in private. A court whose judgment is final precisely because it needs no enforcement. A port administered by a government nobody recognises, where the port authority's account and a presidential post are the only two records. A treasury model that no opposition can audit and no voter can check. In every instance the decisive act happens at the point on the chain where nobody can be held to the evidence — and the evidence, in all five, exists and is not in dispute.
