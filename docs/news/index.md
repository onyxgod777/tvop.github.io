## The Pattern

Look across these five stories. Though they span **a cockpit attack whose custody trail now runs from Oman to Australia while the attacker's name is still unconfirmed**; **a shattered African peace in which a recaptured airfield, a cut diplomatic tie and a rebel's words about the Red Sea have turned an internal war regional**; **a South American election that will decide a forest and test whether a defeated movement accepts a result**; **an Iranian plot against a synagogue on the anniversary of its massacre, and a declared "major incident" at a British airbase**; and **an energy war in which a refinery campaign has brought petrol queues to Moscow and bridge strikes to Kyiv** — the common structure is unmistakable.

**In every one of the five, the event described as news is the *effect*. The cause is upstream, human, and named: a decision to charge one man's violence to a faith and a nation before the file is read; a decision to answer a political dispute with war and to claim a shared sea by force rather than honour signed agreements; a decision to let an election be a proxy for foreign rivalry, a referendum on a convicted predecessor and a prize a forest can be won with; a decision to export the hatred of a war to the streets of another country's cities; and a decision, in both capitals, to fight through the energy and the crossings that ordinary people depend on.**

In every case, the cause is **a human choice** — and therefore, the effect is **changeable**.
