The law of cause and effect is the most precise and impartial of all creational laws. It does not punish or reward — **it reflects**. Every thought, feeling, word, and deed generates a specific vibrational cause. That cause accumulates energy, compresses into lived experience, and returns — amplified — as your destiny.

On this page, we track **current world events as observed causes** and project their **necessary effects** if those causes persist unchanged. This is not fortune-telling. It is **causal inference** — the same principle that lets a physicist predict a trajectory from position and velocity. See the cause, know the effect. Change the cause, and the effect dissolves.

## The Pattern

Read the five together — **an air-defence authorisation that was issued and then expired in cloud**, **a ten-page climate agreement that cannot say oil**, **a trade deal that removes 98.5 per cent of tariffs before the capacity exists to use them**, **a national exam whose score now certifies device detection**, and **a capture that traded eleven lives for one arrest** — and the common structure is not failure and not hypocrisy. It is **the proxy quietly replacing the thing it was supposed to measure**.

**In each case the instrument was calibrated to what could be counted, and the count is not the object.** The alliance counts casualties, so a missile that kills nobody crosses the threshold without triggering it — sovereignty is the object, body bags are the metric. The climate text counts captured tonnes and electricity share, because a fuel cannot be named in a room where producers hold a pen — the atmosphere is the object, the wire is the metric. The trade deal counts tariff lines and modelled GDP, because processing capacity takes a decade to build and a signature takes a morning — resilience is the object, access is the metric. The exam counts detected devices and rank order, because unaided reasoning has no scanner — the intellect is the object, the score is the metric. The raid counts arrests and casualties, because the money layer takes years and a helicopter takes an hour — the network is the object, the body count is the metric.

**And the substitution is not corruption; it is what a veto selects for.** A threshold defined by sovereignty would have to be enforced in weather, by a pilot, on a decision nobody wants to own — so it is defined by casualties instead, which enforces itself. A climate text that names the fuel cannot pass a room containing Saudi Arabia, so it names a technology instead. A trade deal that waited for European refining capacity would never be signed, so it counts tariff lines. An exam that certified unaided reasoning would have to be redesigned by the institutions that depend on the ranking, so it counts seizures. A capture that waited for the financial prosecution would produce no photographs, so it produces arrests. In every instance the measurable proxy is the only form that survives the room it is negotiated in — and the proxy is then presented as the achievement, which is why each of these five was announced as a success.

**The cost lands where the proxy is least able to represent it.** A village fifty kilometres from the border, defended by a rule that depends on visibility. An island state holding a ten-page document that concedes overshoot and cannot name the cause of it. An Indonesian smallholder whose palm oil is governed by an annex written in Brussels and ratified afterwards. A student who sat nine hours alone and now holds a score nobody will trust. Four towns in Sinaloa that were not in the raid and are in the aftermath. None of them is counted by the metric, and all of them are paying for it — because a proxy does not merely approximate the object, it displaces it, and once displaced the object stops being measured at all.
