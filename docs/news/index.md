### 🇮🇷 The War Moves Into the Refrigerators

France has not published how it reached its conclusion, and attribution in cyberspace is genuinely hard.

### 🇸🇳 Thirty-Nine Names, and a Third of an Island

???+ danger "Observed Cause"
    **Thirty-nine people died on a wooden boat between Dakar and Saint-Louis, and the receiving island declared an emergency the day before.** Senegal's interior ministry said **one person was rescued** and that the number still missing had not been established; the boat capsized as it headed for Spain's **Canary Islands**. All the bodies recovered by Sunday evening were Senegalese, according to **Saliou Diouf** of the aid group **Boza Fii**; **24 of the 39** have been identified and returned to their families, **11** await identification. Four more bodies were recovered on Monday in an advanced state of decomposition and buried after a collective funeral prayer by the fire service. Rescue teams are searching a **25 km** stretch of coast, hindered by access and currents pushing bodies south.

    **The island that receives them has run out of the thing it is measured on.** **El Hierro** declared a state of emergency on **Saturday**; more than **3,300 people** have arrived in about **30 boats** since **1 November** — almost **a third of the island's population**. Regional president **Fernando Clavijo** called the situation *"absolutely unsustainable"*. On the same weekend, **UN News** reported at least **56** migrants dead in shipwrecks off **Senegal and Greece**. **Walking Borders** recorded **635 deaths** on the Atlantic route between January and May 2026, and estimates roughly **21 deaths for every 100 arrivals** on the route it monitors. Senegalese authorities intercepted another boat on Friday carrying **142** people — 23 women, two babies and two other children — nationals of Senegal, The Gambia, Guinea-Bissau, Guinea and Nigeria.

    **The push is measurable and it is not being addressed at the point of departure.** Nearly **one in four young Senegalese** was unemployed in the second quarter of 2026, per the national statistics agency. The route has grown because tighter controls in the central Mediterranean diverted journeys west.

??? warning "Projected Effect (if cause persists)"
    - **The island is the unit of measurement, so the emergency will be declared there and the cause will stay here** — a third of El Hierro's population arrived in ten days; the direct effect is that reception capacity, policing and local politics are overwhelmed on a territory of a few thousand people; the compound consequence is that the political response is calibrated to arrivals, not departures — money goes to fences and reception centres at the far end of the route while the employment and network conditions that fill the boats are administered by a different government
    - **"21 deaths per 100 arrivals" is the only honest price on the route, and it is being paid by families who cannot verify it** — 635 deaths in five months, 39 in one boat, one survivor; the direct effect is that the true toll is unknowable because the sea keeps most of it; the compound consequence is that an unverifiable death rate cannot deter anyone — a migrant weighing a 21% risk against near-certain joblessness at home is making a rational calculation, which means the deterrence argument fails on its own terms and the only remaining lever is the one at the departure point
    - **Interception without a legal pathway converts enforcement into a tax on the poor** — 142 stopped on Friday, 3,300 arrived since 1 November; the direct effect is that each interception raises the price and the risk of the next attempt; the compound consequence is that the smuggler's margin grows with the enforcement, so the network the president has pledged to dismantle is funded by the policy aimed at it — the only measure that reduces the margin is a lawful route that undercuts the fee
    - **A receiving island of a few thousand people is being asked to absorb a continental policy failure, and its government is already threatening Madrid** — an "absolutely unsustainable" declaration, Clavijo's legal threats; the direct effect is that solidarity between Spanish regions becomes the next fault line; the compound consequence is that the redistribution of a few thousand people inside one EU state is proving harder than the external border, which tells you what a European relocation mechanism would face — and the delay in Madrid is measured in lives at sea
    - **The sending state's pledge is economic, and its first test is a number it publishes quarterly** — one in four young people unemployed, a promise of "reasons to hope here"; the direct effect is that the credibility of the Senegalese response depends on youth employment, not on patrols; the compound consequence is that a labour market cannot be turned in a season, so the arrivals will continue at or above this rate for at least a year regardless of what is announced — which makes the honest measure of policy the death toll, not the interception count

    *"Provide for (feed and clothe) those who give you signs (evidence) of their poverty (need) so that things may be fair to them and you, and so that no powerful rain of disgust (inhumanity) may sweep down over you which will drown you in shame and guilt, such that you find a miserable end in the estimation of people of your kind (fellow human beings)."* — *Goblet of the Truth*, Chapter 7, Verse 85

    **The hinge:** The cause is not a boat and not an island's patience; it is the gap between a young person's expected lifetime income in Senegal and in Spain, which is large enough that a 21-in-100 chance of dying is a price worth paying. §7:85 makes provision a duty owed to those who show evidence of need, and ties the failure to shame rather than to punishment — that is the correct register for a policy that has been answering a push with a barrier. Neither is dramatic. Both attack the price. A barrier attacks the supply and raises the price, which is why the boats keep coming.

??? warning "Projected Effect (if cause persists)"

    - **The summit in Shenzhen is where a truce is extended, and a truce that only limits escalation is not a settlement** — APEC on 18–19 November, a shaky truce extended ahead of Washington talks in September; the direct effect is that both sides want a deliverable next week; the compound consequence is that the deliverable will be another extension, which leaves the structural surplus untouched — so each summit buys a year of calm and a larger number, and the number is the reason the next summit is needed

    **The hinge:** §4:98 is not an argument against trade; it is an argument about what a deal is *for*, and it names the exact mechanism at work — a profit taken from a partner in a way that prevents friendship is a loss booked in the present and paid in the future. ## The Pattern

Look across these five — **a speaker named before the votes exist**, **talks whose only evidence of movement is an official's word for "flexibility"**, **a sabotage campaign attributed without evidence published**, **thirty-nine dead on a route whose death rate nobody can verify**, and **a trillion-dollar surplus announced a month early** — and the structure this week is not the order. It is **the number**.

**Each story turns on a figure that one party produces and another party cannot audit.** The Israeli bloc publishes a candidate and must produce 61 on a floor where a Haredi party has just declined; the Ukrainian file runs on a private "little bit of flexibility" that no delegation has confirmed; France names the IRGC's Shahid Kaveh and declines to publish the technical basis, while Iran calls it baseless and two Russian-aligned groups claim it and are disbelieved; Senegal's ministry reports 39 dead and says the number missing is not established, while an island of a few thousand counts 3,300 arrivals; and Beijing publishes $1.04 trillion, which Brussels and Washington read as a decision rather than a result.

**The tell is that in each case the party who produces the number chooses the denominator.** A speaker vote is counted in signatures on a request, not in seats on a floor. Flexibility is measured in private assurances, not in a text. An attribution is counted in named individuals, not in evidence. A death toll is counted in bodies recovered along 25 km of coast, not in boats that left. A surplus is counted net, not gross. In every case the choice of denominator is the argument, and the denominator is never on the agenda.

**What this does to causality is precise, and it is the part that matters here.** A cause you cannot measure is a cause you cannot change — not because the will is missing, but because there is no instrument to tell you whether your change worked. That is why §110's instruction is operational rather than moral: change the cause toward the good, and the effect changes; but you can only know you have changed a cause if something counts it. Five countable instruments exist in these five files and all five are idle: a published coalition agreement with signatures; a written paper at the close of the Abu Dhabi round; published technical indicators and one procurement standard for the cold chain; a capped lawful admission channel with a published death toll; a quarterly surplus review and a household-income timetable. None requires a treaty. Each requires a named official to publish a figure they currently prefer not to.
